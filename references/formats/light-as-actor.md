# Light as actor（light-as-actor）

- **Purpose**: Narration (the light has intent) ／ **Granularity×time**: one place × one light event ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: **The light wants something.** It follows, withdraws, waits, refuses — and it does so within the physics of light, so the audience reads intent without being given a spirit.

## Environment variables
`PLACE`＝where the light acts, `SOURCE`＝what the light comes from, `INTENT`＝what the light is doing (following, withdrawing, refusing), `SUBJECT`＝who or what it acts on, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**The light obeys its own law and still has intent.** Sunlight moves because the sun moves; a beam narrows because a gap narrows. ⚠️ **The grammar is that the light's lawful behaviour and its apparent intention coincide** — not that the light breaks physics. **A glow with no source is a ghost, and this card does not make ghosts.**

**One intent per shot.** Following, or withdrawing, or refusing — **not a sequence of moods.** Name the intent in §13, and let the whole shot be one pursuit.

**The subject must be acted upon.** The light's intent is legible because **someone or something is lit or left unlit**. ⚠️ **A shot of beautiful light with no subject is atmosphere**, and atmosphere does not want anything.

**The negative must hold the line.** `no supernatural glow` is the floor: **light answers within the physics of light.** The card's power is that it never needs the exception.

**Distinguish from `meaning-responsive`.** There the world answers *meaning*. Here **one element of the world — light — has a direction**, and it is the shot's only actor.

## do
- Name the light's intent in §13, one intent
- Keep every movement of the light derivable from its source and the place
- Let the subject be lit or unlit — the intent is read on a body
- End on a state the intent has produced (the subject in shadow, or finally lit)

## avoid
- A glow with no source, or a supernatural light that moves by will
- A sequence of lighting moods in one shot
- Light moving over a landscape with no subject it acts on
- A lens flare standing in for intent

## Negative
`no supernatural glow, no sourceless light, no light that moves against its source, no sequence of lighting moods, no lens flare standing in for intent, no lit subject with no reaction to the light, no on-screen text`

## When it earns its place
- **The value it turns.** Being seen becomes being chosen, or being refused — **a body's worth is decided by what lights it.**
- **What the audience loses without it.** The sense of a place that has a will without having a person. **A character can want; only this card lets the room want.**
- **The grounding shot.** A shot of the same place with ordinary, undirected light — **so the intent is measured against light that cared about nothing.**
- **What it costs.** The shot's whole seconds, and the source. **The light may not cheat; it may only be patient.**

## Failure it is confused with
- ⚠️ **A supernatural glow is not this grammar.** It is the failure this card is most often confused with — and the floor already forbids it (`no supernatural glow`).
- **Atmosphere.** Beautiful light with nothing it acts on is a mood, not an actor.
- **A lighting change with no intent** — the sun going down. That is time passing; this card is one thing wanting another.
