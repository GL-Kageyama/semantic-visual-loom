# Physics with a rule（physics-with-a-rule）

- **Purpose**: Narration (an impossible law, stated and paid for) ／ **Granularity×time**: one place × one law ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: **One law of the world is replaced, and the replacement is stated and costs something.** Gravity reverses; water falls upward; a room keeps its own clock. **The rule is what separates this from a picture that simply does not add up.**

## Environment variables
`PLACE`＝where the law is replaced, `LAW`＝the replacement rule, `COST`＝what the rule takes in exchange, `WITNESS`＝who or what shows the rule is lawful, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**One law, stated.** Write the replacement rule in §2's `World Rules` as a sentence the audience could repeat. ⚠️ **A rule nobody could state is not a rule — it is a look.**

**The rule is consistent inside the shot.** Everything obeys it, including the things the audience is not watching. **If one object obeys the old physics, the shot is not surreal, it is broken.**

**The rule costs.** Something is spent, lost, heavy, slow, or irreversible because of it. **A cost is what makes the audience believe the world is real enough to have laws.** Name the cost in §2, and let it be visible in the shot.

**There must be a witness.** A body, an object, a reflection — something in frame whose behaviour demonstrates the law. **An empty landscape under a strange sky is a matte painting.**

**Distinguish from `transformation`.** There an object changes. Here **the world's law changes, and objects stay as they were.**

## do
- State the replacement law in one sentence a viewer could repeat
- Make everything in frame obey it, including the background
- Give the law a cost, and show the cost
- Keep a witness in frame whose behaviour demonstrates the law

## avoid
- A law nobody could state
- One object obeying the old physics — or one ignoring the new one
- An impossible world with nothing paid for it
- An empty frame under a strange sky (no witness, no behaviour)

## Negative
`no unstated rule, no inconsistent physics, no mixed laws in one frame, no gratuitous slow motion, no surreal collage, no floating objects without a law, no on-screen text explaining the rule`

## When it earns its place
- **The value it turns.** What is possible becomes what is real — **and the audience accepts a rule because it was paid for.**
- **What the audience loses without it.** The feeling of learning a world's law inside a shot. **A strange picture shows; this card *teaches*, and the teaching is the pleasure.**
- **The grounding shot.** A shot of the same place obeying ordinary physics, before or after — **so the rule is measured against the world without it.**
- **What it costs.** One law, one cost, one witness. **More than one law in one shot is a look, not a world.**

## Failure it is confused with
- ⚠️ **A surreal collage is not a rule.** If nothing is stated and nothing is paid, the shot is decoration with a strange palette.
- **A physics error** — the render failing. **The difference is the witness and the consistency:** an error is one object; a rule is the whole frame.
- **`transformation`.** If the object itself is what changed, the law did not.
