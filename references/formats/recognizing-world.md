# Recognizing world（recognizing-world）

- **Purpose**: Narration (the setting knows) ／ **Granularity×time**: one setting × folding ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: The place **responds to who is in it** — the world registers the figure's presence, and the registration accumulates rather than happening once.

## Environment variables
`FIGURE`＝who the setting recognises, `SIGN`＝how the setting shows it has noticed, `DEPTH`＝how far the recognition goes, `REFUSAL`＝what the setting will not do, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**This is folding: the recognition accumulates in one frame.** The shot does not cut to a later state of the setting's response — signs stack, and the stacking is the time. A single cut from "unrecognised" to "recognised" is a different grammar; here the frame carries the accumulation.

**The sign is environmental, not social.** Other characters may not turn, greet, or react — that is a scene about recognition, not a setting that recognises. The sign lives in the setting: lights, temperature, doors, screens, growth, water, sound of the place itself.

**The setting has a limit.** `REFUSAL` states what the place will not do even for this figure — the point past which the recognition stops. A setting that does everything reads as an effect; a setting with a floor reads as a place with a character.

**The recognition may be wrong.** The setting can register someone who is not there, or a figure it has mistaken. That is inside the grammar, and it is often the shot's point.

**Distinguish from `meaning-responsive`.** There the world answers what the shot *means*. Here it answers who *is present*. A setting that reacts to a presence it can perceive is this card even when the reaction means nothing.

## do
- Put the sign in the setting — light, temperature, surfaces, the place's own sound
- Let the recognition accumulate across the clip rather than switch on
- State the floor: what the setting will not do
- Keep the other characters from carrying the recognition
- Decide whether the recognition is correct, and let the shot show which

## avoid
- Characters turning, greeting, or reacting in place of the setting
- A single cut from unrecognised to recognised
- A setting that does everything asked of it, with no floor
- A caption or a voice-over announcing that the place knows
- Reading it as weather or mood in general, with no bearer and no sign

## Negative
`no character reacting in place of the setting, no cut between unrecognised and recognised states, no on-screen caption, no voice-over announcing recognition, no blanket mood change, no setting without a floor`
