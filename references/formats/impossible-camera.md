# Impossible camera（impossible-camera）

- **Purpose**: Narration (a viewpoint that cannot exist) ／ **Granularity×time**: one viewpoint × unfolding ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: The shot's viewpoint **cannot exist physically** — the premise that the camera is somewhere is dropped, and the existence of the viewpoint is the event.

## Environment variables
`EYE`＝the viewpoint that cannot exist, `ENTRY`＝how the shot arrives at it, `PATH`＝what the viewpoint travels through, `ARRIVAL`＝where the shot leaves the viewer, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**This is unfolding: the shot is the journey of a viewpoint, and the journey is the time.** This is not a camera move — a camera move keeps the camera somewhere. Here the premise "the camera is at a place" is dropped, and what the shot composes is the *existence* of a viewpoint rather than its movement.

**The entry is stated.** The shot says how it got inside — through an eye, through a wall, into a drop of water, along a wire. An unstated entry reads as a continuity error in the previous shot rather than as this grammar.

**The path has its own law.** Once inside, the world it moves through belongs to that viewpoint, and its scale and physics are its own. Entering an eye and coming out in another world is the grammar's normal case, not a mistake.

**The shot arrives somewhere.** The viewpoint ends where the shot's meaning is. Leaving the viewer inside with no arrival spends the journey and keeps nothing.

**Distinguish from `meaning-responsive`.** Here the viewpoint goes where no camera can. There the viewpoint answers meaning. A camera that moves because a character felt something is that card, even when the move is impossible.

## do
- State the entry — say how the viewpoint got where no camera can be
- Give the path its own law of scale and physics, and keep it consistent inside the shot
- End on an arrival that carries the shot's meaning
- Compose the viewpoint's existence, not the movement of a camera placed at a point

## avoid
- A viewpoint that is merely an unusual camera position
- An unstated entry, so the shot reads as a continuity error
- A path whose physics changes halfway for convenience
- A shot that never arrives anywhere
- Treating it as a camera move with a target and a speed

## Negative
`no conventional camera position, no unstated entry, no cut, no fisheye distortion standing in for an impossible viewpoint, no path that changes its own physics, no shot without an arrival`
