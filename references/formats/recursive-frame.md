# Recursive frame（recursive-frame）

- **Purpose**: Narration (a frame inside the frame) ／ **Granularity×time**: one place × nesting ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: **The picture holds the picture.** A screen, a mirror, a window or a lens inside the frame shows the same scene — and the audience's own position becomes the subject.

## Environment variables
`PLACE`＝where the shot is, `CARRIER`＝what holds the inner picture (a screen, a mirror, a window, a photograph, an eye), `DEPTH`＝how many times the frame repeats, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**The inner frame is not a cutaway.** It is the same place, at the same moment, one step further in. **The shot does not leave the room to show the screen; the screen is in the room.** That is the whole claim: the frame contains itself.

**Depth is finite and stated.** One repetition reads as a mirror. Two read as a system. **Beyond three, the audience stops counting and starts admiring the trick** — say in §10 how deep it goes, and stop there.

**The carrier must be a thing in the world.** A screen, a mirror, a window, a lens, a photograph. ⚠️ **A frame that appears nowhere is not recursion — it is a dissolve with better manners.**

**Something must be wrong inside the frame.** The recursion earns its place when the inner picture shows what the outer one cannot — a hand that is not there, a door that is open, a figure already turned away. **A faithful copy is a trick; a difference is a meaning.**

**Distinguish from `coexisting-realities`.** There, two realities share one place with no order. Here there is one reality, seen twice, **and the pair of views is the subject.**

## do
- Place the carrier visibly in the world before the repetition begins
- State the depth in §10, and hold it
- Make the innermost frame differ from the outermost — one detail
- Let the camera stay outside the recursion; it is the audience's position, not a participant

## avoid
- A frame that appears from nowhere, or an unmotivated picture-in-picture
- More than three levels of repetition
- A faithful copy at every level — nothing to read
- A cut to the inner picture; the point is that it is contained

## Negative
`no unmotivated picture-in-picture, no split screen, no cut to the inner frame, no frame that appears from nowhere, no infinite zoom, no more than three levels of repetition, no on-screen text naming the frame`

## When it earns its place
- **The value it turns.** The audience's place in the picture — **a viewer becomes a subject.**
- **What the audience loses without it.** The moment of being inside a frame that is inside a frame. **No other grammar makes the audience's position the material.**
- **The grounding shot.** A shot of the same place with an ordinary camera, before or after — **so the nesting is measured against a picture that simply shows the room.**
- **What it costs.** One depth, stated. **The camera must not enter the recursion** — the moment it does, the audience's position is taken from them.

## Failure it is confused with
- ⚠️ **A split screen is not recursion.** Two pictures side by side say "meanwhile"; this card says "inside".
- **An infinite zoom** — the frame repeating without an end. That is a screensaver. **Depth is a decision, not a default.**
- **A screen that shows a different scene.** Then it is a cutaway wearing a frame.
