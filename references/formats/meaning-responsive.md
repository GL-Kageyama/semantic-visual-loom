# Meaning-responsive（meaning-responsive）

- **Purpose**: Narration (the image answers meaning) ／ **Granularity×time**: one meaning × a single point ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: The picture **changes because of what it means, not because of what happened** — the world answers the shot's meaning directly, and the answer is the event.

## Environment variables
`BEARER`＝what carries the meaning, `ANSWER`＝how the picture answers it, `DELAY`＝how far the answer lags the meaning, `LIMIT`＝what the answer may not reach, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**This is a single point: nothing in the frame causes the answer.** No object moves into the frame, no light source changes, no one acts. The picture answers the meaning itself. That is the whole grammar, and it is why causality has to be removed from the beat rather than staged.

**The bearer is named, and it does not act.** A face, a held object, a line of text, a silence — the meaning has a carrier in the frame. The carrier is what the picture is answering; the carrier is not what makes the picture answer.

**The answer is in the world, not in the camera.** Rain starts. A colour drains. A surface reflects something that is not there. The camera may not tilt, push, or cut to express it — a camera move is a *statement about* the meaning, and this grammar wants the world to make it.

**The delay is composed.** The gap between the meaning and the answer is the shot's time. Answering instantly reads as a cut; answering at the end of the clip reads as a reveal.

**The limit is stated.** What the answer may not touch keeps it legible. An answer that reaches everything reads as a filter or a grade, not as a response.

## do
- Name the bearer of the meaning, and keep it from acting
- Put the answer in the world — weather, light, surfaces, objects — not in the camera
- Compose the delay between the meaning and its answer
- State what the answer may not reach
- Keep the answer's cause absent from the frame

## avoid
- A camera move, a cut, or a rack focus standing in for the answer
- An actor, an object, or a light source visibly causing the change
- Answering everywhere at once, so the answer reads as a colour grade
- A musical cue or a sound design carrying the meaning instead of the world
- A caption or a voice-over naming what the picture is answering

## Negative
`no visible cause, no camera move answering the meaning, no cut, no rack focus carrying the meaning, no on-screen actor causing the change, no blanket colour grade, no caption naming the meaning, no voice-over naming the meaning`
