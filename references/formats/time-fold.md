# Time fold（time-fold）

- **Purpose**: Narration (a span, held in place) ／ **Granularity×time**: one place × folding ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: Many times pass through **one frame that does not cut away** — the place stays, and what it is made of does not.

## Environment variables
`PLACE`＝the place that holds still, `PASSES`＝what time does to it, `SURVIVOR`＝what does not change across the span, `RANGE`＝the span of time the shot crosses, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**This is folding: the times are stacked into one frame, not laid end to end.** The shot does not cut to a later time — it stays in one place and lets time pass *through* it. Where `transformation` spends its seconds on a change, this spends them on a span: the frame is the container, not the traveller.

**The camera is the anchor.** The place is held. The camera may not cut away and re-enter, because coming back to the place is a second shot and this grammar's whole claim is that it was never left. A fixed frame is the native case.

**One survivor.** Something in the frame does not change across the whole span — that is what makes the passage legible as time rather than as several unrelated states. Name it.

**Uneven duration.** The seconds are not spread evenly over the span; choose the part of the passage the shot lingers on.

**Distinguish from `coexisting-realities`.** Here the times *succeed one another* — one place at many moments. There they coexist and none is earlier. A frame showing two eras with neither arriving first is that card, not this one.

## do
- Hold the place; let the change happen inside a frame that does not cut
- Name the survivor that does not change, and keep it constant
- State the span the shot crosses — years, a lifetime, a season
- Spend the seconds unevenly across the span

## avoid
- A cut, or a return to the place after leaving it
- A date stamp, a caption, or a title card carrying the elapsed time
- Aging makeup, prosthetics, or a stated "years later"
- Every element changing at once, so nothing measures the passage
- Treating the span as several separate shots

## Negative
`no cut, no time-skip, no date stamp, no caption, no title card carrying elapsed time, no aging makeup, no prosthetic, no cross-dissolve, no return to the place after leaving it`
