# Population emergence（population-emergence）

- **Purpose**: Narration (many arrive from the environment) ／ **Granularity×time**: one place × one emergence ／ **Size & aspect**: cinematic 16:9, single clip of `DURATION`
- **Summary**: **A crowd stands up out of the place.** Particles, birds, people, hands — what was ground, air or background becomes population, and the shot's loneliness is answered by number.

## Environment variables
`PLACE`＝where the emergence happens, `SEED`＝what the many come from, `COUNT`＝how many the frame can hold, `ONE`＝the single figure the many answer, `DURATION`＝clip length

## Composition grammar

⚠️ **This card is `video-spec` plus one grammar.** Fill §1–20 exactly as that card requires; what follows is only what this grammar adds to it.

**They come out of the environment, not into it.** The ground lifts, the air thickens, the background detaches. ⚠️ **A crowd that walks in from the edge of frame is not this grammar** — it is a crowd, and it arrives from somewhere else.

**The one is the measure.** A population needs a single figure for the audience to read the number against. **Name the one in §3, and keep it visible** — the shot is about the distance between one and many, and without the one there is only many.

**The emergence stops.** It has a count, an end, a moment when the frame can hold no more. **An emergence that keeps coming is weather.** Say where it stops in §8.

**The camera must give ground.** The many have to arrive into the frame's space — **the shot either pulls back, widens, or holds and lets them fill it.** Say which.

**Distinguish from `scale-rupture`.** There, one subject and one environment exchange roles. Here the environment becomes **many subjects** — and the number is the point.

## do
- Make the many come out of the place itself — ground, air, water, wall
- Keep one figure in frame as the unit of measure
- State where the emergence stops, and stop there
- Let the frame give ground: widen, pull back, or be filled

## avoid
- A crowd entering from the edge of frame
- An emergence with no end — number as texture
- Losing the one figure, so the shot becomes a pattern
- Faces on the many that read as individual characters (the many are a quantity)

## Negative
`no crowd entering from frame edge, no unmotivated emergence, no endless multiplication, no individual faces on the many, no on-screen text counting, no time-lapse`

## When it earns its place
- **The value it turns.** Aloneness becomes multitude — **and the place is revealed as having been full all along.**
- **What the audience loses without it.** The sensation of a quantity arriving. **A cut to a crowd gives the count; this gives the arrival.**
- **The grounding shot.** A shot of the same place with an ordinary camera and no population — **so the emptiness has a measure.**
- **What it costs.** One place and one count. **The many cannot also be characters** — faces on them would spend the shot's meaning on individuals.

## Failure it is confused with
- ⚠️ **Particles as texture.** Dust in a beam is atmosphere; **this card's many have a where-from and a stop.**
- **A crowd scene.** If they walked in, the place did not answer.
- **A time-lapse of growth.** That is `time-fold`'s business; here the many arrive **within the shot's own seconds.**
