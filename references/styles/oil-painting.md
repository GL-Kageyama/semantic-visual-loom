# Oil painting（oil-painting）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Oil painting (classical to modern) ／ **Era**: —
- **Summary**: Thick impasto and strong light-and-dark fix the weight and time of oil on canvas.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Thick brushwork (impasto)
- Raised pigment
- Canvas weave
- Chiaroscuro
- Craquelure, translucent glaze layers
- The hardness of strokes left to dry

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: Chiaroscuro light and dark
- **Typography**: No text
- **Color**: Oil deepened through glazes
- **Texture & light**: Impasto, weave, craquelure, strong light and dark

## Motion character
- **Oil is the slowest mover here — it does not flow, it settles.** What changes in the frame is not
  the shape but the surface: pigment deepening, a glaze sinking.
- **The light does the moving.** It travels across the raised impasto, so ridges catch and release
  and the same stroke reads differently a moment later. The paint stays; the reading of it changes.
- **The camera has weight.** It holds, or moves with the slowness of a heavy dolly. Chiaroscuro is
  spent by a camera that hurries.
- **Stillness is the ground.** A held frame is native to this style — the shot is about what the
  light is doing to a surface that is not going anywhere.
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: flowing liquid motion, contour morphing, fast cuts, digital
  smoothness, motion blur, or a camera that moves for its own sake. ⚠️ **The palette stays deepened
  through glazes** — a colour that flattens or turns bright is a different style.

## do
- Impasto and glazes, light and dark
- Keep the canvas and craquelure

## avoid
- Digital, flat color, airbrush smoothness
- Flowing liquid motion, contour morphing, digital smoothness, motion blur

## Negative
`not digital, no flat color, no airbrush smoothness`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
An oil painting of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}, with {ACCENT}. Thick impasto
brushstrokes, raised pigment, canvas weave, chiaroscuro, fine craquelure, translucent
glaze layers, dry hardened strokes.
```
