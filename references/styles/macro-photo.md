# Macro photography（macro-photo）

- **Medium**: Photography ／ **Lineage**: Macro ／ **Era**: contemporary
- **Summary**: Extreme close-up magnifies texture until the small becomes monumental.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`＝the object, `ACTION`, `LIGHT`＝light, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Extreme close-up, subject fills the frame
- Razor-thin depth of field, the rest dissolves into bokeh
- Enlarged texture and detail, visible materiality
- Macro-lens magnification, true photographic focus
- Soft natural light or a small specular highlight

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: single object centered or offset, magnified to fill the frame
- **Typography**: no text
- **Color**: natural object color, delicate tonal falloff
- **Texture & light**: crisp micro-detail, creamy bokeh behind, subtle sheen

## Motion character
- **The plane of focus is razor-thin, so motion is measured by what stays inside it.** A millimetre
  of travel throws the subject out of the plane entirely.
- **Everything is magnified, including time.** A surface breathing, a bead of moisture, the slow
  crawl of something small — at this scale the smallest movement is the largest event.
- **The camera is the mover, and it moves less than in any other style.** A focus rack, or a few
  millimetres of drift, is a full gesture.
- **Stillness is the ground.** The subject is held in focus and the shot is about what is visible
  while nothing travels.
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: wide movement, travelling shots, articulated full-body motion,
  deep-focus staging, cuts, or a camera that covers distance. ⚠️ **Focus stays thin for the whole
  shot** — a frame that resolves front to back is a different style.

## do
- Magnify the texture until it becomes the subject
- Keep the focus plane razor-thin and the bokeh smooth

## avoid
- Wide shots, flat even lighting, illustrated texture, CGI gloss
- Wide movement, travelling shots, deep-focus staging, camera travel

## Negative
`no wide shot, no flat even lighting, no CGI, no illustration, no painted texture`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A macro photograph of {SUBJECT} {ACTION}, lit by {LIGHT}. Extreme close-up,
a razor-thin plane of focus, the surrounding dissolving into smooth rounded
defocus, enlarged texture and detail, macro-lens magnification, the material's
own color unretouched.
```
