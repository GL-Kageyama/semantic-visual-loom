# Watercolor（watercolor）

- **Medium**: Hand-drawn ／ **Lineage**: Watercolor ／ **Era**: —
- **Summary**: Bleeding washes and translucent layering speak of light while the white of the paper is left to carry it.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `LOCATION`, `ACCENT`, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- wet-on-wet bleeding
- Thin translucent layering (glazes)
- Paper grain and reserved white
- Dry-brush drag
- Contours as pale color edges (never hard ink lines)

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: Reserve the white (paper) boldly; the subject floats on negative space
- **Typography**: No text
- **Color**: Pale and translucent, deepened through layering
- **Texture & light**: Paper grain, bleeding edges, the softness of natural light

## Motion character
- **The water is the mover, not the brush.** What changes between frames is a wash spreading into
  wet paper — pigment blooming outward and settling — never a contour redrawn by a hand.
- **Edges keep advancing.** A wet edge never holds still: the same silhouette is a little different a
  moment later, and it does not return to where it was.
- **The camera is a page, not a window.** The frame holds still, or drifts once and slowly. The
  reserved white *is* what the shot is about, and a travelling camera spends it.
- **Stillness is the ground; the white stays reserved.** The paper's white must survive the whole
  shot — nothing drifts in to fill it (no ambient particles, no haze, no colour arriving).
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: full animation, crisp articulated limbs, contours that hold
  their shape, motion blur, or a camera that moves for its own sake. ⚠️ **The palette stays pale and
  translucent for the whole shot** — a colour that saturates or deepens suddenly is a different
  style, not a beat of this one.

## do
- Reserve white, layer color
- Lean into bleeding and dry-brush drag

## avoid
- Oil impasto, digital smoothness, hard contours
- Hard contours that hold, saturated colour arriving, motion blur

## Negative
`not photorealistic, no oil, no digital, no hard outline`
