# Studio portrait（studio-portrait）

- **Medium**: Photography ／ **Lineage**: Studio portrait ／ **Era**: classic–contemporary
- **Summary**: Controlled light separates the subject from the background and holds their face as a study.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`＝expression / pose, `LIGHT`＝Rembrandt or clamshell light, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- Rembrandt or clamshell lighting
- Shallow depth of field, background separation
- Catchlight in the eyes, sharp focus on the eyes
- Smooth, uncluttered backdrop
- Medium-format film look, fine skin texture

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: single subject centered or slightly off, chest-up or head-and-shoulders
- **Typography**: no text
- **Color**: neutral tones, film color from the chosen light
- **Texture & light**: soft key light, catchlight, fine grain, blurred backdrop

## Motion character
- **The light is set, and it does not change.** Time enters only through the sitter; relighting
  mid-shot is another style's move.
- **What moves is at the scale of a face** — a breath, a blink, the eyes leaving the lens and
  returning. The catchlight is the proof that the light has not moved.
- **The camera is locked on the eyes and holds.** A portrait rig does not wander; if it moves, it is
  a few centimetres of drift at most.
- **Stillness is the ground.** A sitter holding still is this form's native state, and the shot may
  be almost entirely held.
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: walking, gesturing, camera moves, lighting changes, background
  action, cuts. ⚠️ **Focus stays on the eyes** — a frame that shifts its plane to the background is
  a different style.

## do
- Light the face with a clear key / fill relationship
- Keep the eyes sharp and the background soft

## avoid
- Flat frontal flash, messy backgrounds, casual snapshot framing, digital sharpening
- Walking, gesturing, camera moves, lighting changes, background action

## Negative
`no flat frontal lighting, no cluttered background, no snapshot look, no CGI, no illustration`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
A studio portrait of {SUBJECT}, {ACTION}, lit by {LIGHT}. Shallow depth of
field, catchlight in the eyes, sharp focus on the eyes, smooth uncluttered
backdrop, medium-format film look, fine skin texture.
```
