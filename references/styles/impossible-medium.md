# Impossible medium（impossible-medium）

- **Medium**: Mixed ／ **Lineage**: — ／ **Era**: —
- **Summary**: One frame ruled by more than one medium at once — a photograph whose contour is drawn, a watercolour whose light is photographed — so that a property is carried by a medium that cannot carry it, and the disagreement is the image.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`＝the subject, `ACTION`＝action, `LOCATION`＝the place, `ACCENT`＝the property carried by the wrong medium, `ASPECT`＝aspect ratio

## Fidelity anchors
- One frame, two media, and the two do not agree
- A single named property — contour, light, depth, air, motion, expression — carried by the medium that cannot carry it
- The rest of the frame faithful to the other medium
- The seam between them legible: the eye can find where the law changes
- No blend, no average, no transition zone where the two media meet
- A medium with no name — the result cannot be called an existing style

## Visual decomposition
- **Composition**: Composed by one medium; the other one acts inside it
- **Typography**: No text
- **Color**: Each medium keeps its own colour, and the two are not reconciled
- **Texture & light**: Two textures in one frame, meeting at a legible seam

## Motion character
- **The image is one frame ruled by more than one medium, and they do not agree.** Time enters where
  the two laws separate: the photographed light continues under photography's law while the drawn
  contour continues under drawing's.
- **What moves is decided per property, not per object.** A face may hold as a drawing while its
  shadow travels as a photograph — the same object carries two clocks at once, and neither is wrong.
- **The seam is the subject.** Where the two media meet is the shot's event — the place the eye is
  sent, not a flaw to be hidden or a border to be crossed.
- **The camera is a camera.** This style's impossibility is in the image's constitution, not in its
  viewpoint, so the shot frames the subject plainly and the wrongness stays inside the frame.
- **Stillness is not available.** A frame ruled by two laws that disagree cannot rest: at least one
  property is always moving under the law the other medium does not have.
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: settle into one medium, resolve its seam, average the two into
  a blend, feather them together, or let a single clock govern every property. ⚠️ **The disagreement
  must survive the whole shot** — a frame that resolves into consistency is a different style, not a
  beat of this one.

## do
- Name the property that is displaced, and displace exactly one
- Keep every other property faithful to the medium that owns it
- Keep the seam legible — the disagreement must be findable by the eye
- Let both laws run at once, each on its own property

## avoid
- Blending the two media into an average, or feathering one into the other
- Transition zones and gradients where the media meet
- Displacing more than one property at a time (that is a blend, not this style)
- Naming the result as an existing style
- A frame that resolves into one consistent medium
- Motion blur, morphing, or a camera move for its own sake

## Negative
`no blended media, no averaged style, no transition zone, no gradient between media, no single consistent medium, no named existing style`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
{SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {LOCATION}. One frame ruled by two media that do not agree:
{ACCENT} is carried by the medium that cannot carry it, while everything else in
the frame stays faithful to the other medium. No blend, no average, no transition
zone between them; the seam where the law changes stays legible. Not describable
as any existing style.
```
