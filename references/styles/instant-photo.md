# Instant photo（instant-photo）

- **Medium**: Photography ／ **Lineage**: Instant film ／ **Era**: 1970s–90s
- **Summary**: A white-bordered frame and on-camera flash keep a moment as a kept memory.

## Environment variables
`SUBJECT`, `ACTION`, `SCENE`＝the place, `LIGHT`＝flash, `ASPECT`

## Fidelity anchors
- White instant-film border framing the image
- On-camera flash, direct and slightly hard
- Faded or slightly oversaturated vintage colors
- Slight softness, mild blur, imperfect alignment
- Instant film texture, subtle chemical mottling
- A nostalgic keepsake, a private moment

## Visual breakdown
- **Composition**: casual snapshot, subject centered under flash, border frame
- **Typography**: no text (a handwritten date may sit in the border)
- **Color**: faded warmth or oversaturated pop, flash-cooled skin
- **Texture & light**: direct flash, soft grain, white border

## Motion character
- **The print is a surface, and its chemistry is what moves.** The image surfaces, mottles and
  settles slightly — the change belongs to the emulsion, not to the subject.
- **The flash does not change.** It is the light of the whole shot; a subject may move under it, but
  the light never relights.
- **The frame is a kept object.** The white border holds and the picture never leaves it; the border
  is not a device to be crossed.
- **The camera does not travel.** An instant photograph is a thing held in a hand — the only drift
  available is the small unsteadiness of holding it.
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: studio relighting, crisp continuous action, sharp articulated
  movement, colour grading, cuts, or a camera that goes anywhere. ⚠️ **The colours keep ageing for
  the whole shot** — a colour that stabilises and turns accurate is a different style.

## do
- Keep the white border and the direct flash
- Let the colors age into a kept memory

## avoid
- Studio lighting, professional color grading, crisp digital sharpness, CGI
- Studio relighting, crisp articulated action, camera travel, colour stabilising

## Negative
`no studio lighting, no professional color grading, no crisp digital sharpness, no CGI, no illustration`

## Prompt template (English, fill-in-the-blank)
```text
An instant photograph of {SUBJECT} {ACTION} in {SCENE}, taken with on-camera
flash. White instant-film border, faded vintage colors, slight softness and
mild blur, instant film texture, direct flash lighting.
```
