## 主題

- `REF_FORMAT`: `art-board` —— 4つの穴（`LOCATION`／`HOUR`／`LIGHT_SOURCE`／`DIRECTION`）
- `REF_STYLE`: `one-lamp-cel` —— 5つの穴（`SUBJECT`／`ACTION`／`LOCATION`／`LIGHT`／`ACCENT`）
- `LOCATION`: the boundary crossing at night — two rails, black sleepers, gravel and earth with two wheel ruts, white and black barrier arms, no pavement, no sky
- `HOUR`: an hour without a clock — no time of day, no sky, no sun, no moon, no stars
- `LIGHT_SOURCE`: the soul-fire, a flame no larger than a plum, floating low above the rails, held by no one
- `DIRECTION`: from the low left, along the rails toward the camera
- `SUBJECT`: the crossing and the soul-fire
- `ACTION`: floating low and still above the rails
- `LIGHT`: one small pale blue-white fire from the low left, and nothing else
- `ACCENT`: the rust-red rim of the fire, the rust on the iron, and the wet gravel doubling the light once

## 検品（絵が戻ったら見る）

- 錆が鉄に在るか。レール・継目板・ボルト・腕木の縁・錘。色は錆の赤で、橙・黄・金になっていないか。

- レールが銀色に光っていないか。
