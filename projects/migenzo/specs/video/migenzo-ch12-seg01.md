# 1. VIDEO

- Duration: `9s`
- Aspect: `16:9`
- Resolution: `1920x1080`
- Frame Rate: `24fps`
- Orientation: `Landscape`
- Generation Intent: One continuous take, with no cut in it, and no dissolve and no wipe. The last darkroom, at the light island: a tray of amber liquid whose surface is now still, and beyond the reach of the dim red safelight the room's faces sunk in the darkness that has been settled along those walls for twenty years — and the edge where the red gives way to that darkness is held inside this frame, because that edge is what shows the paper is no longer inside the liquid's world. Above the tray one wet silver-gelatin sheet hangs in the air, held at one corner by a pair of tongs that come in from outside the frame, with no person anywhere in the shot. On the sheet the last of the image is going: a small hand, nearly gone, the outline thinning at its edges. In these nine seconds the sheet does not move at all — not a millimetre — and the camera does not move either. What happens is three things and only three: the last of the image returns to plain white, and two drops of water gather at the lower corner and fall and then stop falling, and a band of the room's own red light runs across the white while the white takes a little of it and stays white exactly where it is. The paper is still wet at the end and never dries, and nothing is decided in the white: it does not say love and it does not say detention and it does not say the answer, and it will not change again, because the paper is out of the liquid now. This is the last frame of the work.

## Style Motion

Full animation, not limited — and the form's native state is available to it, and this is the last shot of the work, so it spends the most of any shot on it. **The moment is unstaged, so time is whatever the subject does while unaware.** Nothing here is arranged for the camera: a sheet has come out of a liquid and hangs drying in the air, and the picture does not stage the resting of it. **What moves is ordinary and small** — two drops of water, and a white returning to plain white, and the room's own red light running along it — and **the frame does not arrange itself around the movement**: it holds the island and its edge at the same size, because that edge is what shows the paper has left the liquid's world, and it keeps holding them after the water has stopped. **The camera stands and watches, at eye level.** It holds; if it moves at all it moves to keep the subject in frame, never to make a point — and here moving in would push the island's edge out of the shot and make the camera the thing that goes to read the white, which is the one thing this shot must not do: the white has nothing to read and has to be left as it is. **Stillness is fully available.** An unstaged moment may simply continue without anything happening, and that is not a failed shot — it is the form's native state: six of these nine seconds are exactly that, and the last three are the work's own last frame, and a camera that moved in them would signal that something more is still coming, and nothing more is. **Nothing is performed for the camera, and nothing is repeated for it**: the water falls twice and not a third time, and the light runs along the white once, because a repeat would be a performance. ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: posed performance, staged lighting changes, dramatic camera movement, cuts to a second angle, a grade that shifts mid-shot, motion for its own sake — none of it is here: nothing dims, nothing brightens, no colour shifts and no new source arrives, and what runs along the white is the room's own state and not a lighting change.
