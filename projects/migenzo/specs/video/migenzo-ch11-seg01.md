- Camera Language: 三人称。**最後の暗室で立っている人の眼の高さ。**
  ⚠️ **枠のなかに、液のなかの濡れた印画紙がある**——
  **背中と、レンズを押さえる小さな手と、顔があるはずの場所の白。**
  ⛔ **そして、紙の手前の角に、絹の4本の指がある**——

  ⚠️ **この2つ（像のなかの小さな手と、紙の角の4本の指）が、同じ枠に同居する**——
  **§18 は、この2つを別々のものとして書く。**
  ⛔ **絹の体も、顔も、この枠に入らない。**
- Camera Events: `0-3s` none——**像と、白と、紙の角の指に留まる。**
  `3-7s` none——**赤い光が、白のなかへ入り、横切る。**
  ⛔ **カメラは、光を追わない。** **光は、枠の内側を、端から端まで通る。**
  `7-8s` none——**光が白の外へ出て、とまる。** ⛔ **ここで寄らない。**
  ⚠️ **この8秒で、カメラの位置は1ミリも変わらない。**

  ⚠️ **この1本に固有の理由**——`shots/migenzo-ch11-seg01.yaml` の `law`——
  「**寄れば、それは「白を確かめる」画になる**——**この1本は、確かめない。**」

# 18. WAN 3.0 PROMPT MAPPING

## Camera Prompt

Third-person, at the height of a person standing at the tray in the darkroom, looking down into the liquid at a wet print lying in it: a back turned away and running, a small hand stopping the round black mouth of a lens, and, where the face should be, a white area whose edge dissolves into the rest of the image with no line drawn between them. Four fingertips of a hand rest on the near corner of the paper in the foreground, the outermost little finger lifted slightly clear of the corner, with red light passing under it — these fingers belong to the person standing at the tray, and they are a different hand from the small one inside the image. The camera does not change position at any point in the eight seconds: no push, no pull, no pan, no tilt, no rack focus, no handheld, no shake. [0-3s] holding on the print, the white and the small hand in it, and the four fingers at the corner, with nothing moving. [3-7s] still holding as a band of red light comes in from one side and crosses the white area slowly, and inside that crossing something like the shape of a face seems about to appear and does not, and the hand inside the image and the fingers at the corner both stay exactly where they are. [7-8s] still holding as the light leaves the white and stops, and nothing is left behind it, and the white is one white with no edge in it. The white is not resolved in this shot, and coming closer would make the camera the thing that goes to check it: the light is the only thing that travels here, and it leaves nothing behind. The style permits the camera to move to keep the subject in frame; this shot spends none of it, because the frame already holds the whole of what is being looked at. One continuous take; no cut.
