# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

2026, Japan. A public elementary school in Tokyo, and the staff room of it. There is no magic, no institution and no secret organization — there is only a daily life in which the etiquette of the written name is thoroughly in place. **The name on paper is the older of the two names in this work, and it is the one nobody erased.**

## World Rules

- **The people in the work do not know that they are inside a film.**
- **The hand comes first and the face comes after. The order is not swapped. The face is placed only after the name has been called.**
- **The sound is not a calling voice; it is the sound of paper and hands.**
- **Outside the work, for the first time, someone calls out. Inside the work it is never spoken. Being spoken outside does not cancel being unspoken inside. The cost is kept, not erased.**
- **This work places no one in frame but her.** The caller and the listener are outside the frame.
- **No text is burned into this work.**
- **No name is spoken in this work.** No voice is placed at all.
- **This work does not decide whether the four beats are the same day.**

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime — **the light, not the figure, is the subject.** Here the light has to do the thing the work cannot do in words: it makes the paper visible and its writing unreadable, by putting the sheet at an angle to the key and letting the page's surface carry the glare.
- Color Language: Saturated where the light falls, deep cyan in the unlit half. Paper white becomes the brightest surface in the work; the plastic highlight of the earlier shots is gone.
- Texture: The register's page takes the light along its grain and along the ruled columns; the ink sits in the paper rather than on it. No paper texture *effect*, no grain, no painterly stroke — the sheet must read as a photograph of paper inside an anime, not as a filter.
- Rendering: Hyper-detailed layered light — bloom at the tube, dust suspended above the desk, clean anime lineart on the figure and the desk **kept subordinate to the light.**
- Visual Density: Low. One page, one figure, and the distance between them.
- Time: `2026年の勤務日の日中` — a working day, daytime, fluorescent.
- Atmosphere: A desk in a working room, and a person standing at the edge of it.

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

- Timing Policy: `STRUCTURED` / `NON_UNIFORM`
- Temporal Sequence:
  - BEAT 1 `0-4s` — density: `sparse` — The open register on the desk: **the page, the transfer column, the line that was not erased.** She stands in front of it. Nothing is legible.
  - BEAT 2 `4-7s` — density: `held` — **Half a step back. And it does not finish.** The weight moves to the back foot. **Only paper and hands are audible.**
- Temporal Density: The held beat is the one where almost nothing moves, and it takes the larger share of the shot. **The distance is the content.**

# 9. ACTION

- `ACT_STAND` — Before: she is not at the desk. After: she is standing in front of the open register. **Not shown; the shot opens on it.**
- `ACT_STEP` — Before: her weight is even. After: **her weight is on the back foot and the step is not finished.** The action has no end state in this shot.
- `ACT_ASK` — Before: nothing has been said. After: the question has been put, silently, to no one in frame. **The source's one consistent reaction, and it produces one movement.**

# 10. CAMERA

- Camera Language: Third person, at the height of the desk and slightly to the side — **the register and the distance between it and her are in the same frame, because the shot is that distance.** The style's light-first hierarchy holds: the paper is the brightest surface and the figure stands in relation to it.
- Camera Events: One event only. `0-7s` — a slow, weighted settle that follows the step **without closing the gap.** The camera does not move toward the page.
- Camera Behavior: No handheld, no whip, no shake, no cut, no rack focus to the writing. One continuous take.

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

**One movement: half a step back.** Slow, small, taken on the back foot, **and stopped before completion.** Her upper body stays upright; her face does not perform.

## Object Motion

**None.** The page does not turn. The register does not slide. Nothing on the desk is touched.

## Environmental Motion

Dust falls continuously through both beats and keeps falling after the step stops. The light holds. **The dust over the desk is the only thing moving at the end of the shot.**

## Physical Characteristics

- Weight: **The step has weight** — the weight transfer is visible in the hips and in the back leg, and it is what makes this a body rather than a slide.
- Inertia: The step carries a little and is stopped by the back leg. **It is not a glide and it is not a stagger.**
- Acceleration: One slow acceleration, then a deceleration that does not reach zero before the cut.
- Fluidity: Continuous. No snap, no stutter, no popping between poses.
- Impact: None.

# 12. EMOTION

- Emotional Arc: The moment of standing in front of a name that is not yours and not being able to stand closer.
- Emotional Events: The instant the step stops short.

# 13. LIGHTING

- Base Lighting: Fluorescent key from above the frame's edge, falling on the open page and along the desk edge. The page's writing sits at an angle to the key so that the surface carries the glare and the ink does not resolve. Deep cyan in the unlit half of the room and on the unlit side of her. Dust suspended above the desk, in the light.
- Lighting Events: One — as she steps back, the lit page becomes a larger share of the frame and the light on the paper becomes the frame's centre. **The step is what promotes the page.**

# 14. AUDIO

- Sound Effects: **Paper and hands** — the register's page in a dry room, the small creak of a desk, the shift of her weight on the floor. Nothing else is mixed forward.
- Music: **None.** No score, no BGM, no drone, no pad, no sting, no swell.
- Environment: An office interior at work; a fluorescent hum. Nothing musical.

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

- No romaji in place of the Japanese name.
- No calling voice as a sound effect; no audible speech; no spoken name; no voice-over; no narration.
- No face before the name is called.
- **No completed action** — **in this shot: the half step does not finish**, and the weight does not settle.
- No smile, no tears, no fear, no pleading, no exaggerated expression.
- No wall clock, no calendar, no digital timer, no date stamp.
- No cardigan, no jacket, no blazer, no outer garment.
- No identifying clothing, hairstyle or prop; no signature; no handwriting by the subject.
- No uniform pacing, no equal-length beats, no static slideshow of stills, no floaty weightless motion, no scene cuts to unrelated locations.
- No watermark, no on-screen subtitles, no captions.
- Not photorealistic, no 3D render, no muted desaturated palette, no flat gradient sky, no grain, no painterly brush strokes, no photographic faces.

## MUST

- **The step does not complete.** She stops with the weight on the back foot and stays there through the cut.
- **The page is in frame and cannot be read** — the columns, the line, all of it present and unresolved.
- The only movement is the step; **the page does not turn and nothing on the desk is touched.**

## PREFER

- The register and the distance between it and her held in one frame, with the paper white as the brightest surface.

## ALLOW

- The desk's near edge in the foreground, out of focus, at the bottom of the frame.

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The half step does not finish** — an uncompleted movement is the shot. A finished step would end the work early.
2. **The writing does not resolve** — no legible characters, no invented name, no pseudo-kanji that reads as a name. **Present and unreadable.**
3. **The page is not the shot's only subject** — the distance between her and it is. Either alone is a different shot.
4. **One movement only** — the step. No second gesture, no pen, no page turn.
5. **No second person** — the question is put to no one in frame.
6. Everything else.
