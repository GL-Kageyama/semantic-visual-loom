# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

2026, Japan. A public elementary school in Tokyo, and the staff room of it. There is no magic, no institution and no secret organization — there is only a daily life in which the etiquette of the written name is thoroughly in place. **The name on her chest was not written by her.** She reads it with her fingers, and she does not say it.

## World Rules

- **The people in the work do not know that they are inside a film.**
- **The hand comes first and the face comes after. The order is not swapped. The face is placed only after the name has been called.**
- **The sound is not a calling voice; it is the sound of paper and hands.**
- **Outside the work, for the first time, someone calls out. Inside the work it is never spoken. Being spoken outside does not cancel being unspoken inside. The cost is kept, not erased.**
- **This work places no one in frame but her.** The caller and the listener are outside the frame.
- **No text is burned into this work.**
- **No name is spoken in this work.** No voice is placed at all.
- **This work does not decide whether the four beats are the same day.**

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime — **the light, not the figure, is the subject.** Here the light has a single job: to make cloth readable as cloth. The subject of the shot is a hand, and the light is what gives the hand something to move on.
- Color Language: Saturated where the light falls, deep cyan in the unlit half. The palette narrows further than in the first shot — almost everything in frame is cloth, skin and plastic.
- Texture: The cloth takes a fine grain of light along its weave; the dust stays in the air behind the hand. No paper texture, no painterly stroke, no film grain.
- Rendering: Hyper-detailed layered light — bloom at the tube, the hand's edge lit from above, the shadow of the hand moving on the cloth and being the second thing that moves in the frame. Clean anime lineart on the hand, **kept subordinate to the light.**
- Visual Density: The lowest in the work. One hand, one surface, one focal point.
- Time: `2026年の勤務日の日中` — a working day, daytime, fluorescent.
- Atmosphere: Private, and not hidden. She is doing this where anyone could see, and no one is there.

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The pin does not close** — the act does not complete. This outranks a satisfying gesture.
2. **The characters are never seen** — they are under the cloth. Showing them, even for a frame, ends the work's premise.
3. **The hand is the subject** — the face does not take the shot, even when it is permitted in frame.
4. **The stop is the content** — the finger stops and the hand stays. Withdrawing the hand resolves what this shot withholds.
5. **This is the work's centre** — where a shot must be sacrificed in a longer edit, it is not this one.
