# 1. VIDEO

# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

2026, Japan. A public elementary school in Tokyo, and the staff room of it. There is no magic, no institution and no secret organization — there is only a daily life in which the etiquette of the written name is thoroughly in place. **And at the end of this twenty-eight seconds, for the only time, the person in the frame looks back.**

## World Rules

- **The people in the work do not know that they are inside a film.** ⚠️ **This is the one rule this work exempts, in this shot and nowhere else.** **She looks at the lens.** The other four shots hold the rule; this one is the seat the rule left open — run-10:71: outside the work, for the first time, someone calls out, and the call is never spoken inside.
- **The hand comes first and the face comes after. The order is not swapped. The face is placed only after the name has been called.** ⚠️ **The order holds here**: she was called in the first shot, and the face has been in frame since. **The look is not a second calling; it is a reply.**
- **The sound is not a calling voice; it is the sound of paper and hands.**
- **Outside the work, for the first time, someone calls out. Inside the work it is never spoken. Being spoken outside does not cancel being unspoken inside. The cost is kept, not erased.**
- **This work places no one in frame but her.** The caller and the listener are outside the frame. **Here, the outside is the lens.**
- **No text is burned into this work.**
- **No name is spoken in this work.** No voice is placed at all.
- **This work does not decide whether the four beats are the same day.**

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime — **the light, not the figure, is the subject.** In this shot the light has to carry a face that is doing nothing, for five seconds, without becoming a portrait: the key comes from above and at the frame's edge, the shadow side holds, and the dust between the lens and the face is what keeps the stillness from going flat.
- Color Language: Saturated where the light falls, deep cyan in the unlit half. This is the shot where the lit half and the shadow meet on her face and stay there.
- Texture: Layered depth; the room behind is lit blur and nothing more. No grain, no paper texture, no painterly stroke.
- Rendering: Hyper-detailed layered light — bloom at the tube, particles suspended between the lens and the face and individually rendered, clean anime lineart on the face **kept subordinate to the light.**
- Visual Density: The lowest in the work together with the second shot. **One face and nothing to look at but it.**
- Time: `2026年の勤務日の日中` — a working day, daytime, fluorescent.
- Atmosphere: Quiet, and not intimate. **She is not offering anything; she is looking.**

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

- Timing Policy: `STRUCTURED` / `NON_UNIFORM`
- Temporal Sequence:
  - BEAT 1 `0-3s` — density: `held` — **She does not move.** One half step back from the paper, gaze on it. **These three seconds are a wait.**
  - BEAT 2 `3-5s` — density: `held` — **The gaze lifts and stops on the lens.** The expression does not change: no smile, no fear, no question. **She only looks.**
- Temporal Density: Both beats are held, and the second one is the change. **The shot is mostly not-happening, and that ratio is the design.**

# 9. ACTION

- `ACT_WAIT` — Before: nothing. After: nothing. **Three seconds of a person standing still is an action in this work, and it is written as one.**
- `ACT_LOOK` — Before: her gaze is on the paper. After: her gaze is on the lens. **The single change of the shot, and the only exemption in the work.**

# 10. CAMERA

- Camera Language: Third person, at her height, close — the frame is her face and the shadow side of it. **The lens is what she looks at, so the camera's position is the shot's content and not its craft.** It does not comment on being looked at: **no push-in, no rack, no reaction.**
- Camera Events: One event only. `0-5s` — a very slow, weighted settle of a few centimetres, ending before the gaze arrives. **Nothing about the camera acknowledges the look.**
- Camera Behavior: No handheld, no whip, no shake, no cut, no zoom. One continuous take.

# 11. MOTION

⚠️ **`mode: still` である。** この節は空ではない——**動くのは視線であり、止まっているのは主題である。**

## Subject Motion

**The gaze.** From the paper up to the lens, slowly; **the stop is longer than the movement.** Her head moves only as much as the gaze requires. Her expression does not change: no smile, no fear, no question. **She does not blink as punctuation.**

## Object Motion

None. The register is out of focus at the frame's edge and does not move.

## Environmental Motion

Dust falls continuously through both beats. The light keeps its smallest unevenness. **Both keep moving while she does not — this is what carries a five-second still shot without a held frame.**

## Physical Characteristics

- Weight: **The head has weight and does not float.** Even a few degrees of movement reads as a body holding itself up.
- Inertia: The gaze arrives and stays; it does not overshoot and correct.
- Acceleration: One slow acceleration and a long stop.
- Fluidity: Continuous and slight. No snap, no stutter, no held cel.
- Impact: None.

# 12. EMOTION

- Emotional Arc: The one moment in the work where the person in the frame and the person watching are in the same room.
- Emotional Events: The instant the gaze arrives on the lens and does not leave.

# 13. LIGHTING

- Base Lighting: Fluorescent key from above and at the frame's edge; the lit side of the face warm, the shadow side deep cyan. Dust suspended between the lens and the face, in the light. Bloom at the tube, above and out of the way.
- Lighting Events: One — as the gaze lifts, the lit side of the face turns very slightly toward the key, and the edge of the light moves across the cheek and stops. **The light change is a consequence of the look, not a comment on it.**

# 14. AUDIO

- Dialogue: None. **The name is still not spoken, and the look does not speak it.**
- Sound Effects: **Paper and hands** — the same bed as the rest of the work, at its quietest. Nothing is added for the look.
- Music: **None.** No score, no BGM, no drone, no pad, no sting, no swell. ⚠️ **特にこのショットで、音楽を置いてはならない**——置けば、この視線が「感動の瞬間」になる。**見るだけである。**
- Environment: An office interior at work; a fluorescent hum. Nothing musical.

# 15. CONTINUITY

- Identity: The frozen pair, unsummarised, attached on every instance. **No drift; this is the frame the audience will remember her by.**
- Spatial: She is still one half step back from the register, and does not step further. The register is at the frame's lower edge.
- Temporal: This shot does not fix the date, and nothing in frame supplies one.
- Visual: Same palette, same fluorescent key, same dust as the other four shots.
- Motion: Full animation, not limited — **the style does not become limited for a still shot.** The atmosphere is the primary mover; here the atmosphere is dust in front of a face.
- Sound: Paper and hands. No voice, and no music.

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST NOT

- No second person in frame — no caller, no listener, no colleague, no passer-by, no hand but hers. **The one who is being looked at is the lens.**
- No legible text on any surface — the register's columns, the line in it, the nameplate.
- No romaji in place of the Japanese name.
- No calling voice as a sound effect; no audible speech; no spoken name; no voice-over; no narration.
- No face before the name is called.
- No completed action, no finished movement, no resolved gesture.
- **No smile, no tears, no fear, no pleading, no exaggerated expression.** ⚠️ **This is the shot where that line is most likely to be broken** — the look must not be given a feeling.
- No wall clock, no calendar, no digital timer, no date stamp.
- No cardigan, no jacket, no blazer, no outer garment.
- No identifying clothing, hairstyle or prop; no signature; no handwriting by the subject.
- No uniform pacing, no equal-length beats, no static slideshow of stills, no floaty weightless motion, no scene cuts to unrelated locations.
- No watermark, no on-screen subtitles, no captions.
- Not photorealistic, no 3D render, no muted desaturated palette, no flat gradient sky, no grain, no painterly brush strokes, no photographic faces.

## MUST

- **The gaze arrives on the lens and stays.** It does not flick away, and it is not held back shyly.
- **The expression does not change.** She looks; she does not perform looking.
- **The dust keeps falling** for the whole five seconds.

## PREFER

- The look arriving late in the shot, out of three seconds of stillness — the wait is what makes the change countable.

## ALLOW

- The open register, out of focus, at the bottom edge of the frame.

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The expression does not change** — no smile, no fear, no question, no tear. **This outranks every other consideration in this shot.**
2. **The look is not returned by the film** — no push-in, no music, no bloom swell. The camera does not notice.
3. **The face does not drift** — the identity lock holds; this is the last frame of the work.
4. **One change only** — the gaze. No step, no blink as punctuation, no breath taken visibly.
5. **Stillness is not a still image** — the dust must move throughout, or the shot reads as a photograph.
6. Everything else.

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

- **The expression may be given a feeling.** The strongest pull in the whole work is here: a smile, a held breath, a glint. **She looks, and nothing else.** This is the first thing to check on the returned clip.
- **The camera may acknowledge the look.** A push-in, a rack to her face, a bloom swell — any of them turns the exemption into a technique. **The exemption is hers, not the camera's.**

- **The shot may become a still image.** Dust must move throughout, or the five seconds read as a photograph.
- **A second person may be invented** — the temptation to show who is being looked at. **There is no one; the lens is the outside.**
- **The look may arrive too early.** If it lands in the first beat, the wait disappears and with it the countability of the change.
