# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A mountain pass at dusk. The road switches back across the slope; the sky is
still lit and the valley has begun to go blue. **There is no other place in this
work** — this road is the whole world of it.

## World Rules

- **The light is the subject.** Sky, valley and wet asphalt move more than the figure does.
- **The figure stays small and subordinate to the light.** The face is never close.
- **The lantern is unlit.** It is carried, and it does not burn in this shot.
- **Nothing is explained.** No caption, no sign, no voice names this place.
- **Nothing supernatural happens.** The light answers within the physics of light.
- **No legible text** appears anywhere.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime — layered skies with individually shaped clouds, volumetric god rays through the air, anamorphic flare and bloom around the source, a saturated dusk of magenta and gold against deep cyan shadow.
- Color Language: Magenta and gold in the lit half; deep cyan in the shadow. **No third family of hue.**
- Texture: Particles suspended and falling through the beams; wet asphalt doubling the sky.
- Rendering: Clean anime lineart on the figure, **kept deliberately subordinate to the light.** Not photorealistic, not a 3D render.
- Visual Density: Low near the figure, high in the sky.
- Time: 夕暮れ
- Atmosphere: Still, and already over.
