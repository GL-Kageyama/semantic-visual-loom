# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A mountain pass at dusk, at the bend where the road turns the shoulder and the
valley opens below. **This is the place the rest of the work returns to.**

## World Rules

- **The light answers her.** The sun drops a step and the valley floor stands up in light — **the world registers her, and it does so by brightening.**
- **The world does not grieve.** It will not dim, will not grey out, will not mourn. That refusal is the grammar's floor, and this shot's meaning.
- **Nobody names the place.** No caption, no sign, no voice.
- **She does not react in place of the world.** No gesture, no turn, no step back.
- **The lantern stays unlit.**
- **No legible text** appears anywhere.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime — layered skies with individually shaped clouds, volumetric god rays through the air, anamorphic flare and bloom around the source, a saturated dusk of magenta and gold against deep cyan shadow.
- Color Language: The palette **brightens as the shot proceeds** — the shadow half loses cyan and gains gold as the valley lights up. **This is the only colour event.**
- Texture: Particles lift as the light angle changes; the wet asphalt begins to take the valley's light.
- Rendering: Clean anime lineart on the figure, subordinate to the light. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render.
- Visual Density: Low before the bend, high after it — **the frame fills as the valley enters it.**
- Time: 夕暮れ
- Atmosphere: Beautiful, and entirely unwilling to apologise.

# 3. SUBJECTS

## The Girl

- Reference: (none — not identity-locked; **her face is never shown close**)
- Appearance: Seen from behind. White shirt, dark trousers, paper lantern in the right hand, **unlit.**
- Behavior: She walks to the bend, turns, and **stops.** She does not go further. She does not look down.
- Continuity Requirements: Must Preserve: white shirt, dark trousers, lantern in the right hand, **her back to the camera.** May Change: her position relative to the bend, the height of the lantern hand.

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

She walks to the bend, turns, and stops. **The stop is the last movement of her body in this shot.** The lantern hand lowers by a few degrees. Nothing else.

## Object Motion

The lantern swings on the way up and settles when she stops. **It emits nothing.**

## Environmental Motion

The sun visibly drops a step; the shafts swing; particles lift **upward** out of the beams; the valley floor's light mounts, and the road takes it. **The world's motion is the shot's motion.**

## Physical Characteristics

- Weight: The light has mass — the valley floor rises like water finding a level.
- Inertia: The light keeps moving after she has stopped; it is still moving at the cut.
- Acceleration: The change is a step, not a fade — **one beat, and the world is brighter.**
- Fluidity: Air, then water, then stone, in that order of brightness.
- Impact: The gap in the rail, entering **inside** the light.

# 12. EMOTION

- Before: She is walking toward something she has not seen yet.
- Pressure: The place is beautiful, and she knows what it is.
- Turn: **The world brightens at the exact place where it should not.**
- After: She cannot move, and nothing in the world asks her to.
- Button: **The broken guardrail inside the light.**

# 16. CONSTRAINTS

## MUST NOT（この1本の禁止）

- No close-up of a face.
- No crying, no gesture of grief, no hand to the mouth.
- No lit lantern.
- No readable text of any kind.
- No character reacting in place of the setting — **the recognition belongs to the light.**
- No cut between an unrecognised and a recognised state.
- No caption or voice-over announcing that the place knows.
- No blanket mood change — **the world brightens; it does not turn sad.**
- No camera move.

## MUST

- The stop comes **before** the light changes.
- The gap in the guardrail enters the frame **inside the light**, not before it.
- She does not move after stopping.

## PREFER

## ALLOW

- Particles lifting upward as the shafts swing.
