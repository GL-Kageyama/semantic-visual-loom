# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

The same mountain pass, seen from where no camera can stand — **through the gap in
the guardrail, down the slope, into the valley floor.** The world below is the
world above, at a distance that removes its light.

## World Rules

- **The viewpoint is not a camera.** No body holds it, no rig carries it; it goes through the gap and down.
- **Light obeys distance.** Near the sun it is bright; the valley floor is dark. **This shot uses that honesty as its turn.**
- **The path's law does not change halfway.** It descends steadily; it does not swoop, orbit, or return.
- **Nothing supernatural appears.** No glow, no spirit, no sign.
- **Nothing is left down there.** No car, no remains, no object — **only wet rock and the sky's reflection.**
- **No legible text** appears anywhere.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime — volumetric shafts seen from inside them, anamorphic bloom above, deep cyan accumulating downward.
- Color Language: **Gold and magenta only in the upper third.** The descent removes them a step at a time; the valley floor is cyan, wet grey and black. **The palette loses its warm half.**
- Texture: Particles in the beams; wet rock, moving water, broken branches.
- Rendering: Clean anime rendering, never photorealistic, not a 3D render.
- Visual Density: High in the beams, sparse at the floor.
- Time: 夕暮れ
- Atmosphere: Beautiful at the top, and honest at the bottom.

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

No figure. **The slope is passed through** — rock and scrub slide by, near and far, establishing depth without being touched.

## Object Motion

Broken branches and water at the floor. **Nothing is displaced by the viewpoint.**

## Environmental Motion

Particles move **upward** while the viewpoint goes down — the shallows of the light. The beams are crossed from the side, and their angle changes as the viewpoint drops below them.

## Physical Characteristics

- Weight: The descent has mass; it commits, it does not float.
- Inertia: Once moving, the viewpoint does not correct.
- Acceleration: Near-even; **the brightness accelerates away faster than the motion does.**
- Fluidity: Air, then rock, then water.
- Impact: None — **the arrival is not a landing; it is a stop.**

## Anticipated risks (to check in the first generation)

1. **The camera may become a drone.** A descending viewpoint reads as a camera
   move unless the entry is stated and the path keeps its own law. **The entry
   line in the `Camera Prompt` is doing the work.**
2. **Something may appear at the floor.** The model will try to reward the descent
   with an object. **The arrival must stay empty.**
3. **The viewpoint may look back.** Any return to her breaks both the grammar and
   the shot after it.
4. **The brightness may fall smoothly.** The steps are the argument; a smooth fade
   reads as sunset rather than as distance.
