# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A bronze-age Mediterranean island shore at the end of the day, and the open sea beyond it. **No place in this work is named aloud.** The island holds a cave and a goddess who lives in it; a man has been on this shore for years and has not been able to leave it. ⚠️ **This is a theme-song music video:** one song of 299.920 seconds, thirty-four shots, no episode — **the work does not advance a story. It holds one man at the edge of one island and asks the same question with other light.** ⚠️ **The poem is older than classical Greece, and the picture is built that way**: rough stone, wood, coarse cloth and bronze; no marble, no columns, no temple. ⚠️ **この1本で、この作品は初めて「ここに無いもの」を画の中へ入れる。** **入れ方は一つしかない**——**同じ一枠に並べ、どちらも先にしないことである。** ⛔ **「向こう」を「ここ」の未来にすれば、この作品は帰還の物語になる。** **この1本は、それをしない。**

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Open sea at the end of the day, photographed as a film — **the water is the whole of the picture and the land is not in it.** Long low swells with no white water, moving steadily in one direction; the surface broken by weed passing and by nothing else; the horizon level and unbroken. ⚠️ **No sail, no bird, no other vessel, and no land on the horizon.** ⚠️ **The man is of this place and not above it** — one coarse worn tunic, bare feet. ⚠️ **No marble, no columns, no architecture of any later age** — **and no house is built in this frame: the roof-form is in the water and nowhere else.**
- Color Language: A narrow, graded palette — cold slate blue in the water, and the one warm ochre where the low sun falls. ⚠️ **One grade for the whole frame.** **「ここ」と「向こう」を別の色で塗り分けない**——**塗り分ければ、二つの現実は二つの画になり、この1本は二つの現実を持たなくなる。** **ゆえに区別は、色ではなく、形と動きで作る。**
- Texture: The water's surface fine-grained and broken, with weight and visible movement in every part of it; the reflected light broken into short strokes rather than a mirror; coarse wool with visible fibre on the near shoulder. ⚠️ **No shingle in this frame and no beach** — **岸はこの画に無い。** Film grain present and even.
- Rendering: Photographic — anamorphic optics, a moderate depth of field, subtle oval bokeh, a gentle flare where the light is in frame. **Not a photograph's stillness: a film frame, with a real lens's fall-off at the edges.** No illustration, no CGI look, no cartoon color.
- Visual Density: Low to moderate, and **even** — there is one plane, the surface, and it carries everything. ⚠️ **密度を上げるのは形であって、物ではない。****新しい物は何も入らない。**
- Time: `日没` — the last of the day, on the open water. **The same evening as `s01`–`s06`; the work does not fix a date.** ⚠️ **この1本の「向こう」は、別の時刻ではない。****同じ一日の終わりである**——**ゆえに時間差は無く、あるのは場所の差だけである。**
- Atmosphere: The minute in which a man finds the far shore in the water in front of him rather than on the horizon.

# 11. MOTION
