# 1. VIDEO

- Duration: `18.000s`
- Aspect: `16:9`
- Resolution: `1920x1080`
- Frame Rate: `24fps`
- Orientation: `Landscape`
- Generation Intent: One continuous take of one change — **the evening goes through one frame and the boundary between sea and sky disappears.** At the first frame the sun is still above the water and the sea is one colour; by the last frame the sun is below it, two stars are out, and there is no line where the water ends. ⚠️ **The frame never leaves the place and never cuts** — the span is stacked into one image, not laid end to end. ⚠️ **No person is in this shot, at any distance, in any focus.** ⚠️ **The change completes on the last frame of the take** — the boundary goes at the cut — **and the frame is never still in the meantime**: the swell arrives at the same rate from the first frame to the last, and that rate is the only thing the evening does not touch.

# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

The open water off a bronze-age Mediterranean island, at the hour the day ends, seen from the island's own waterline. **No place in this work is named aloud.** The island holds a cave and a goddess who lives in it; a man has been on this shore for years and has not been able to leave it. ⚠️ **This shot holds none of that** — it holds water, light and sky, and the passing of one evening across them. ⚠️ **This is a theme-song music video:** one song of 299.920 seconds, thirty-four shots, no episode — **the work does not advance a story. It holds one man at the edge of one island and asks the same question with other light.** ⚠️ **The poem is older than classical Greece, and the picture is built that way**: rough stone, wood, coarse cloth and bronze; no marble, no columns, no temple.

## World Rules

- **The people in the work do not know that they are inside a film.**
- **The answer is never given.** The goddess offers and the man says nothing. **A shot in which he opens his mouth is a shot this work does not have.**
- **The goddess is never a face.** She is a voice from off-frame, a warmth on surfaces, moving air, and a light on the water that comes from behind the camera. ⚠️ **She is not in this shot in any of those four forms** — the light here is the sun's own, and it is going.
- **No proper name is ever seen or heard.** Not on screen, not in the song.
- **The bow does not appear in this work.** The axe is inside the fifth book; the bow is outside it.
- **This work does not arrive.** The raft is still offshore in the last frame of the last shot.
- **No text appears on any surface.** The work names things by voice; it does not write them. ⚠️ **This is the rule this shot is most likely to break** — see §16.
- **The age of the poem is bronze-age Mediterranean, before classical Greece. It does not move.** ⚠️ **This shot crosses eighteen seconds and no era.**
- **Only so many places can stand at once.** This shot places one, and **does not leave it.**
- **This work speaks Japanese.** It is the language of the song, and there is no other voice in it.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: The open sea seen from an island waterline at the day's end, photographed as a film. ⚠️ **There is very little in this frame that a person made** — water, sky, cloud, and the sun's own light. The sea is the largest object and it is the whole of the lower frame; the horizon is the only line in the picture and **by the last frame it is gone.** No marble, no columns, no architecture, and no vessel of any kind — **there is nothing on this water.**
- Color Language: The palette **is the shot's change**, and it moves in one direction: from the warm orange of a sun still above the water, through the flat grey of a sky with the sun taken out of it, to a cold blue that holds two points of white. The water goes from one colour to a colour it cannot be told apart from the sky. ⚠️ **The change is stepwise, not a fade** — the beat is a colour arriving and staying, not a dissolve.
- Texture: Open water with a fine-grained, broken surface; cloud with soft, uneven edges; a film grain even over everything. ⚠️ **No glassy water, no smooth gradient sky, no airbrushed sunset.** No painterly stroke, no plastic surface.
- Rendering: Photographic — anamorphic optics, deep focus on the water and the cloud, subtle oval bokeh, a lens flare permitted while the sun is in the frame and **lost once it is below the water.** Not a photograph's stillness: a film frame, with a real lens's fall-off at the edges. No illustration, no CGI look, no cartoon color.
- Visual Density: **Low, and lower as the shot goes on.** One focal point — the sun's path on the water — which shortens, breaks up and is then gone; the frame then holds nothing but water, cloud and two stars. **The emptiness at the end is the picture.**
- Time: `日没` — the end of the day, from the sun still above the water to the sea and sky losing their boundary. **The same evening as `s01`, `s02`, `s04`〜`s08`; the work does not fix a date.**
- Atmosphere: A place watched by nobody, at the hour when the light stops being able to be ignored, until there is no light left to ignore.

# 7. NARRATIVE

- Core Event: **The evening goes through one frame and the boundary between sea and sky disappears** — the sun touches the water, sinks, the colour of everything goes down stepwise, two stars come out, and the line the picture was built on is gone.
- Beginning: **The sun is still above the water and the sea is one colour.** The swell is already running at the rate it will keep; the reflection is short and the sky is warm.
- Turn: **The light goes oblique.** The reflection lengthens into a band running toward the camera, the cloud crosses right to left, and the sky's colour begins to settle rather than shift.
- Peak: **The sun touches the water and the band breaks.** The reflection becomes a path and then a scatter of moving cells, and the sky goes from orange to grey — **not faded, arrived.**
- Pull: ⚠️ **The boundary between sea and sky goes on the last frame.** Two stars are already out above it, the water can no longer be told from the sky, **and the shot hands the cut a picture with no line in it** — because the next thing the work says is a voice.

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

- Timing Policy: `STRUCTURED` / `NON_UNIFORM`
- **Time fold（形式カード `time-fold` の5つの変数。⚠️ 定義はカードの逐語である）**:

  - `PASSES`＝what time does to it — **the light goes down through the water and the sky.** 日が水面に接し、一本の道ができて砕け、空の色が橙から灰へ移り、星が二つ出て、**海と空の境が消える。**
  - `SURVIVOR`＝what does not change across the span — ⚠️ **ひとつだけ名指す。波の寄せる間隔である。** 18秒のあいだ、**寄せては返す速さは一度も変わらない。** 光は沈み、色は落ち、雲は渡り、星が出て、境は消える——**そのすべてを測る物差しが、この一つである。** ⚠️ **「境」を生存者にしてはならない**——**境が消えることが、この1本の変化である。**
  - `RANGE`＝the span of time the shot crosses — **夕のひとつ分である。** 日が水面の上にあるところから、海と空の区別が薄れるところまで。⚠️ **年でも一生でも季節でもない**——**この作品が畳むのは、一日の終わりである。**
  - `DURATION`＝clip length — **`18.000s`。**
- Temporal Sequence:
  - MOVEMENT 1 `0-3.996s` — density: `transition` — **日がまだ高い。** 水面は一つの色で、影は短く、反射は輪のようである。⚠️ **画面は止まっていない** — 波は同じ間隔で寄せている。

- Temporal Density: **Uneven, and weighted to the last nine seconds.** ⚠️ **形式カードの逐語: 「Uneven duration. The seconds are not spread evenly over the span; choose the part of the passage the shot lingers on.」**——**この1本が長く居るのは、日が水に接してから沈みきるまでである。** 頭の4秒は夕の幅を測るために払う。⚠️ **`held` を1つも使わない。** 止まるのは主題であって、画面ではない（`shot-record.schema.json` の `motion`）。

# 9. ACTION

- `ACT_HOLD` — Before: the place is fixed and the swell is running at its rate. After: **the same, and this never changes.** ⚠️ **これは「何もしない」ではない**——**この1本の物差しが、ここで立てられる。**
- `ACT_FALL` — Before: the sun is above the water and the light is high. After: **the light is oblique and the reflection has lengthened into a band.**

- Camera Language: Third person, **standing at the waterline with the sea filling the frame to the horizon.** ⚠️ **形式カードの逐語:「The camera is the anchor. The place is held. … A fixed frame is the native case.」**——**この1本は、その本来の場合である。**

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

**The sea, at a rate that does not change.** Long low swells arrive and go back on one interval for the whole eighteen seconds. **This is the survivor**, and it is the layer the shot is measured by.

## Object Motion

**無し。** ⚠️ **この1本には物が無い。** 動くのは水と雲と光だけである。

## Environmental Motion

**The cloud, right to left**, at a rate that also does not change, and **without thinning into a shape or gathering into a mass.** **The light itself, going down** — the reflection shortening, breaking, and being extinguished. **The stars, arriving** — one, then two, and they do not move once they are out.

## Physical Characteristics

- **Weight**: **Water is heavy and slow; the light on it is weightless; the sky is neither.** ⚠️ **三つの速さが違うことが、この1本の内容である** — 波は同じ間隔で重く、雲はゆっくり渡り、光だけが速く沈む。
- **Inertia**: **The swell overshoots and returns**; the cloud keeps its direction and does not stop; **the colour, once it has arrived, does not go back.** ⚠️ **この1本の中に、戻る変化は一つも無い。**
- **Acceleration**: **Constant in the water and the cloud; uneven in the light.** ⚠️ **色の移りは加速しない**——**段として来る。** 沈む直前だけ、**光の道が砕ける速さが上がる。**
- **Fluidity**: Continuous; no snap, no held cel, no stutter. ⚠️ **この18秒に、止まったフレームは一つも無い**——**波は18秒のあいだ一度も止まらない。**
- **Impact**: **None.** Nothing in this shot strikes anything. ⚠️ **それは運動の不在ではない** — 環境の運動の項を見る。
