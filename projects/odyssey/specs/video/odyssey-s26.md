## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Open sea at night, photographed as a film frame **from the raft's own bow, facing forward**: the near ground holds the lashed logs of the bow and the water breaking at their edge, and beyond it **black water runs unbroken to a level horizon.** ⚠️ **No land, no sail, no bird, no other vessel** (逐語, `ledger.locations.海.base`). ⚠️ **No made thing is in this frame except the raft's own bow** — no oar is visible in it, no rope is being worked, and no light is carried. **No marble, no columns, no architecture of any later age.**
- Color Language: A narrow, graded palette — **the waves are black and the only white in the frame is one broken path of reflected starlight on the surface.** The sky is one shade above the water and carries the stars. ⚠️ **The shot has no second light and no warm source**（`海.states.夜` の註の逐語:「光源は星と、その水面の反射だけである。波は黒く、反射の道だけが白い。」）。⚠️ **The colour is the same in the first frame and the last: nothing is revealed, and nothing lights up.**
- Texture: The bow under the frame: pine grain and bark, coarse hand-twisted cordage carrying its uneven twist, hewn plank ends, and the wet edge where the water comes over. **The water is long low swells with no white water**, moving steadily in one direction, fine-grained and broken only by the reflected path and by weed passing. ⚠️ **No spray, no breaking crest and no foam anywhere in this frame** (逐語, `海.base` の `no white water`). Film grain present and even.
- Rendering: Photographic — anamorphic optics, a moderate depth of field, subtle oval bokeh, a gentle flare on the one reflected path. **Not a photograph's stillness: a film frame, with a real lens's fall-off at the edges.** No illustration, no CGI look, no cartoon color.
- Visual Density: Low, **and it stays low for all 11.091 seconds** — この1本は、この作品でいちばん何も無い画である。⚠️ **ビートの `dense` は、物が多いことではない。****この1本でいちばん多くが起きているということであり、それは「何も現れなかったことが確定する」ことである。**
- Time: `夜` — the night of the bridge, on the same water as `海` since `s18`. ⚠️ **この1本は、歌の終わりから始まる1.091秒を持つ**（239.787–240.878）——**この作品で、歌が止まっている区間が画面に残る、数少ないコマである。**
- Atmosphere: The hour in which the song promises a destination and the picture refuses to produce one — **and the refusal holds for the whole take.**
