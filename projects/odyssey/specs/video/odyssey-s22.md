## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Open sea at night, photographed as a film frame, **from a little further out than the first time**: the near water fine-grained and broken and running, the horizon level and unbroken and still, and nothing between the two of them but the surface itself. **The raft is low in the near ground, small, with one man's back in it and his left hand on the lashed oar.** ⚠️ **No land, no sail, no bird, no other vessel.**
- Color Language: A narrow, graded palette — black water, a sky one shade above it, and one white path of reflected starlight running from the near surface out to the far line. ⚠️ **One light and one grade: the near and the far are the same colour** (形式カードの `Negative` の `no color grade separating the realities`)。**空気遠近で色を変えない。**
- Texture: Fine-grained broken water in the near ground, going smooth with distance — **the same surface, read at two scales.** Barked pine logs, coarse hand-twisted cordage and undyed wool where the raft and the man are; skin roughened and marked. Film grain present and even. ⚠️ **近景と遠景のあいだに、質感の切れ目を作らない。**
- Rendering: Photographic — anamorphic optics, **the focus held so that the near water and the far horizon are both sharp**, subtle oval bokeh, the faintest flare on the reflected path. **No split screen, no wipe, no dividing line drawn anywhere in the frame.** Not an illustration, not a collage: **one lens, one exposure, one frame.**
- Visual Density: Low, and it **decreases** over the shot: the frame starts with water, a horizon and a raft, and settles into water, a horizon and one mark. ⚠️ **遠くなるほど、画は薄くなる。**
- Time: `夜` — the source is the stars and their reflection on the water. **The waves are black and the reflected path alone is white** (逐語, `ledger.locations.海.states.夜`). ⚠️ **この1本の二つの遠さは、同じ一つの時刻の中にある。****どちらも先ではない。**
