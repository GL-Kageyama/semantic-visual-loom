# 1. VIDEO

- Duration: `10.500s`
- Aspect: `16:9`
- Resolution: `1920x1080`
- Frame Rate: `24fps`
- Orientation: `Landscape`
- Generation Intent: One continuous take of one change — **the last of the day's light finishes arriving on the water.** At the first frame the sea has no colour and the shore is grey; by the last frame a path of light has formed on the surface, broken into a moving net, and **the ground has become legible as grain** — sand, dry weed, small stones. ⚠️ **No person is in this shot, at any distance, in any focus.** ⚠️ **The change completes on the last frame of the take** — the dry weed is still rolling when the cut arrives — **and the frame is never still in the meantime**: the swell is always arriving and going back, and the light on it never settles.

# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A bronze-age Mediterranean island shore at the end of the day, and the open sea beyond it. **No place in this work is named aloud.** The island holds a cave and a goddess who lives in it; a man has been on this shore for years and has not been able to leave it. ⚠️ **This shot holds none of that yet** — it holds the shore, the water and the light, and it is the first thing the work shows. ⚠️ **This is a theme-song music video:** one song of 299.920 seconds, thirty-four shots, no episode — **the work does not advance a story. It holds one man at the edge of one island and asks the same question with other light.** ⚠️ **The poem is older than classical Greece, and the picture is built that way**: rough stone, wood, coarse cloth and bronze; no marble, no columns, no temple.

## World Rules

- **The people in the work do not know that they are inside a film.**
- **The answer is never given.** The goddess offers and the man says nothing. **A shot in which he opens his mouth is a shot this work does not have.**
- **The goddess is never a face.** She is a voice from off-frame, a warmth on surfaces, moving air, and a light on the water that comes from behind the camera. ⚠️ **She is not in this shot in any of those four forms** — the light here is the sun's own.
- **No proper name is ever seen or heard.** Not on screen, not in the song.
- **The bow does not appear in this work.** The axe is inside the fifth book; the bow is outside it.
- **This work does not arrive.** The raft is still offshore in the last frame of the last shot.
- **No text appears on any surface.** The work names things by voice; it does not write them.
- **The age of the poem is bronze-age Mediterranean, before classical Greece. It does not move.**
- **Only so many places can stand at once.** This shot places one.
- **This work speaks Japanese.** It is the language of the song, and there is no other voice in it.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: A bronze-age Mediterranean island shore at the end of the day, photographed as a film. Rough wet stone, coarse sand, low dense scrub, sea-worn driftwood; undyed wool and stiff salt cloth, a bronze double-axe with a patina, a raft of unseasoned timber and a sail of hand-sewn cloth. **The sea is the largest object in every frame that contains it.** Light is the sun's own — low, raking, and going down. No marble, no columns, no architecture of any later age.
- Color Language: A narrow, graded palette — cold slate blue in the water and the wet stone, warm ochre where the low sun falls, and a single band of pale gold on the water where the light comes down it. The shore is desaturated until the light reaches it; **the palette is what the shot's change moves through**, and it moves in one direction only, from grey to gold.
- Texture: Wet shingle has grain and individual stones; the dry weed is fibrous and hollow; the water surface is fine-grained and broken, never glassy and never smooth. Film grain is present and even. No painterly stroke, no airbrush, no plastic surface.
- Rendering: Photographic — anamorphic optics, shallow depth of field with the background carried as atmosphere, subtle oval bokeh, a gentle flare when the light is in frame. **Not a photograph's stillness: a film frame, with a real lens's fall-off at the edges.** No illustration, no CGI look, no cartoon color.
- Visual Density: Low. One focal point — the path of light on the water — with the near ground holding two or three readable objects (a rolled piece of weed, a flat stone, a bare driftwood limb) and the rest of the frame given to water and air.
- Time: `日没` — the last of the day, outdoors, on the island's shore. **The same evening as `s04`〜`s08`, the four other shore shots; the work does not fix a date.**
- Atmosphere: A shore that is being looked at by nobody, at the hour when the light stops being able to be ignored.

# 3. SUBJECTS

## The shore, the water and the light

- Reference: `岸` ／ `岸.geography` ／ `岸.states.日没` — **the ledger's sentences are the reference.** ⚠️ **No image is attached**: this route attaches nothing by default, and this work attaches nothing by choice (裁定②). **The identity lock is the paragraph in §15**, not a picture.
- Appearance: The waterline runs left to right across the near edge of frame, with coarse dark sand and wet shingle below it and open water beyond and away from the camera. Low wet rocks at the water's edge; a rising bank of dense low scrub behind, **and it is not entered**. The sea fills the upper half of the frame. The horizon is level and unbroken.
- Behavior: **The water is the mover.** Long low swells come in and go back without breaking into white water; where they touch the shingle they lift the dry weed and leave it, and the weed rolls once and stops. The light on the surface changes faster than the surface does.
- Continuity Requirements: **Must preserve** — the waterline, the horizon, and the height of the sea in frame; the sand is coarse and dark, the shingle is wet, the scrub is low and dense and has no path through it. **May change** — the surface, the light, and everything the water is doing. ⚠️ **The horizon stays level in every shore shot of this work** (`岸.geography`); the camera is never canted.

# 7. NARRATIVE

- Core Event: **The last of the day's light finishes arriving on the water** — a path forms on the surface, breaks into a net, and the ground becomes grain.
- Beginning: **The sea is one colour and the shore is grey.** The swell is already running and the camera is already moving; nothing in the frame has a shadow yet.
- Turn: **The light comes down the sea.** A single band of pale gold appears on the surface, running toward the camera and widening, and **the near ground takes its first shadow.**
- Peak: **The path breaks into a net.** The band shatters into moving cells that do not hold their shape, the water's colour goes from slate to something darker and warmer at once, **and the sand reads as grains.**
- Pull: ⚠️ **The dry weed is still rolling when the take ends.** The light is one step from gone, **and the shot hands the cut a picture that is still changing** — because the song's first voice arrives on that frame.

# 8. TEMPORAL STRUCTURE

- Timing Policy: `STRUCTURED` / `NON_UNIFORM`
- Temporal Sequence:
  - MOVEMENT 1 `0-1.501s` — density: `transition` — **the picture stands up out of a dark blue.** The water is visible first and the shore cannot yet be told from it; the surface is one colour, the horizon is not yet a line. ⚠️ **The frame is not still while this happens** — the swell is already running and the camera is already travelling.
  - MOVEMENT 2 `1.501-5.995s` — density: `sparse` — **the surface and the sand.** The camera runs low, just above the water, moving right; the swell passes under it and the shingle slides through the near ground. Nothing has changed colour yet. **This is the longest stretch of the take and the least eventful**, and it is where the shot earns the right to its ending.
  - MOVEMENT 3 `5.995-10.5s` — density: `dense` — **the path of light.** The band forms, widens, breaks into a net and keeps breaking; the near ground goes from grey to grain; **the dry weed rolls once across the shingle and comes to rest — and the take ends on that frame.** ⚠️ **The cut arrives on the roll's last movement, not after it.**
- Temporal Density: **The weight of the take is at its end**, in one continuous arrival of light that does not stop before the cut. ⚠️ **Ten and a half seconds without a song, and the picture runs the whole way** — because the song's first line lands at 52.899s, thirty thousand frames later, and **a picture that has already stopped cannot receive it.** ⚠️ **There is no `held` movement in this shot.** 止まるのは主題であって、画面ではない（`shot-record.schema.json` の `motion`）。

# 10. CAMERA

- Camera Language: Third person, **low and near the water** — the lens sits a hand's width above the surface, so that the sea fills the upper frame and the shingle slides through the near ground. **This is the work's first camera position and it establishes the height the four shore shots are shot from.**
- Camera Events: One event only. `0-10.5s` — **a continuous travelling move to the right with a slow push**, at a rate that does not change, **still running on the last frame of the take.** ⚠️ **The move is motivated: it is following the path of light down the water.** ⚠️ **This is the camera the whole introduction runs on** — no cut stops it inside this take, and `s02` begins from a new position, which is why `s01` has to be one unbroken move.
- Camera Behavior: **The style permits a dolly, a crane and a Steadicam, and this shot spends the Steadicam** — the travel is smooth and weighted, with the low frequency of a rig that has mass, and it does not wobble. No handheld, no whip, no shake, no unmotivated move. ⚠️ **Do not stop the move before the cut.** No sudden zoom, no rack focus, no unnatural rotation. ⚠️ **The composition is allowed to change** — the shingle leaves the frame and the open water grows in it as the camera travels.

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

**The sea.** The swell runs in and goes back on a steady interval; the surface is never still and never repeats a shape.

## Object Motion

**The dry weed, once** — it rolls a short way across the shingle and stops, and **the take ends before it is fully at rest.** **The water's band of light, continuously** — it widens, breaks into cells, and the cells move faster than the water they sit on. ⚠️ **The stones do not move.**

## Environmental Motion

Fine sand lifts a hand's width above the shingle in the last third and crosses the near ground; the low scrub behind the beach moves in the moving air, all of it at once and without a gust. ⚠️ **This is the layer the shot runs on**, not a decoration on a still.

## Physical Characteristics

- **Weight**: **Water is heavy and slow; light on water is weightless.** ⚠️ **The two rates are the shot** — the swell moves with mass and the band on it does not.
- **Inertia**: **The swell overshoots and returns.** The dry weed, once moved, keeps going further than the water that moved it, and stops late. ⚠️ **Nothing in this frame stops at the moment it is released.**
- **Acceleration**: **Constant in the water, uneven in the light** — the band's cells accelerate as the cells break, and the breaking is not even across the frame.
- **Fluidity**: Continuous; no snap, no held cel, no stutter. ⚠️ **There is no held frame anywhere in this take** — the opening 1.5 seconds are a movement of water and camera, not a still.
- **Impact**: **None.** Nothing in this shot strikes anything. ⚠️ **That is not the absence of motion** — see Environmental Motion.

# 12. EMOTION

- Emotional Arc: **The interval before anything is asked** — the work shows the island with nobody on it, for as long as it takes the light to arrive.
- Emotional Events: ⚠️ **The event of this shot is not an expression and not a figure.** It is **the arrival of the light itself**, and the shot does not celebrate it. **The emotion is in the flatness of the treatment: a first image, given no more room than any other.**

# 13. LIGHTING

- Base Lighting: The sun's own light, low and behind the camera's left, raking across the water; no fill, no artificial source, and a lens flare permitted where the band is in frame. The water is the brightest thing in the frame at the end and the greyest at the start; **the sky is not asked to be a second light.**
- Lighting Events: **One, and it is the shot's change.** The path of light comes down the sea and forms between 5.995s and the cut; the shingle takes its first shadow at 6.5s and the near ground's texture becomes visible from there. ⚠️ **This is not a lighting event in the sense of a lamp being switched** — nothing is switched, and the sun does not move perceptibly in ten seconds. **What changes is what the surface does with the light it is already receiving.** ⚠️ **The grade holds for the whole shot** (様式カード) — 色温度が振れるのは別の様式である。

# 14. AUDIO

- Dialogue: **None.** No line is spoken in this shot, and **no one is in it to speak one.** ⚠️ **This is not a silent shot** — see Sound Effects. ⚠️ **The work still speaks Japanese**; §18 names it.
- Sound Effects: **Water and shingle, nothing mixed forward.** The low continuous sound of a swell arriving and going back; the dry click of small stones turning over in the wash; ⚠️ **and the absence of a voice** — nothing in this shot is called, and the absence is placed rather than left out.
- Music: **None, by specification.** ⚠️ **This is not an omission** — §16 and §18 both carry the floor's `no background music`, and ⚠️ **on this route `No BGM` is one of the few negations that is actually received.** ⚠️ **And it matters more here than anywhere: the work's only music is the song 『永遠より遠い』, placed in post over this shot** — a generated bed would put two musics on the same ten seconds. There is no bed, no score, no sting, and nothing rises at the cut.
- Environment: An open shore at the end of the day, with the sea in the near ground. Wind in low scrub, water on shingle, and no other source. **No voice of any kind** — and here there is not even a person for one to come from.

# 15. CONTINUITY

- Identity: **Must preserve** — the waterline, the horizon, the coarse dark sand, the wet shingle and the rising bank of scrub are the same in all five shore shots of this work, and **this shot is where they are established.** ⚠️ **The camera height of this shot is the work's shore height** — `s04`〜`s08` are shot from it. **May change** — nothing about the place.

The palette is the shot's own, and so is the light.** **The light is the sun's own** — the work's five shore shots share it.

# 18. SEEDANCE 2.5 PROMPT MAPPING

⚠️ **この経路の §18 は、他の二つの経路と書き方が違う。** **時区分をプロンプトの中に書ける**（`0-3s:`）——

⚠️ **そして `Negative Prompt` を、この経路は床として受け取らない**（`specmap.MODEL_UNRECEIVED_SLOTS`）。

## Style Motion

- **The frame is one frame of a take, and the take does not cut.** Time enters as continuation, not as a second setup — a cut would make it two stills.
- **What moves was already in the frame.** The camera's slow travel, a flare crossing the lens, a held gesture completing: nothing is introduced that the still did not imply.
- **Shallow depth is the mover's constraint.** What leaves the plane of focus loses itself, and coming back into it is the beat — the shot's changes happen at the focus plane.
- **The camera moves with a real rig's weight** — dolly, crane, Steadicam — or holds. But a hold here is a decision a camera makes, not a photograph's stillness.
- ⚠️ **What this style does not do**: cuts to a second setup, flat television lighting, snapshot framing, handheld wobble, unmotivated camera moves. ⚠️ **The grade holds for the whole shot** — a colour temperature that swings is a different style.
