# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A bronze-age Mediterranean island shore at the end of the day, and the open sea beyond it. **No place in this work is named aloud.** The island holds a cave and a goddess who lives in it; a man has been on this shore for years and has not been able to leave it. ⚠️ **This shot is where he enters** — the first person the work shows, on the same shore `s01` established, at the same hour. ⚠️ **This is a theme-song music video:** one song of 299.920 seconds, thirty-four shots, no episode — **the work does not advance a story. It holds one man at the edge of one island and asks the same question with other light.** ⚠️ **The poem is older than classical Greece, and the picture is built that way**: rough stone, wood, coarse cloth and bronze; no marble, no columns, no temple.

## World Rules

- **The people in the work do not know that they are inside a film.** ⚠️ **This is why he does not look at the lens** — the camera is not there for him.
- **The answer is never given.** The goddess offers and the man says nothing. **A shot in which he opens his mouth is a shot this work does not have.** ⚠️ **This shot is his first appearance and his mouth is closed.**
- **The goddess is never a face.** She is a voice from off-frame, a warmth on surfaces, moving air, and a light on the water that comes from behind the camera. ⚠️ **She is not in this shot in any of those four forms** — **there is one person here, and it is the man.**
- **No proper name is ever seen or heard.** Not on screen, not in the song. ⚠️ **The song's first line names him by nothing** — 「日のあるかぎり岸に出て」 says what he does, not who he is.
- **The bow does not appear in this work.** The axe is inside the fifth book; the bow is outside it. ⚠️ **He carries nothing in this shot.** 手は空である。
- **This work does not arrive.** The raft is still offshore in the last frame of the last shot.
- **No text appears on any surface.** The work names things by voice; it does not write them.
- **The age of the poem is bronze-age Mediterranean, before classical Greece. It does not move.** ⚠️ **He is not dressed as a classical hero** — no armour, no helmet, no greaves, one coarse undyed wool tunic, bare feet.
- **Only so many places can stand at once.** This shot places one — the same shore as `s01`.
- **This work speaks Japanese.** It is the language of the song, and there is no other voice in it. ⚠️ **The song begins on this shot's last frame**, and the song is Japanese.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: A bronze-age Mediterranean island shore at the end of the day, photographed as a film. Rough wet stone, coarse sand, low dense scrub, sea-worn driftwood; undyed wool and stiff salt cloth. ⚠️ **The man is of this place and not above it** — his one tunic is coarse, worn through at the shoulder seam, and belted with a plain leather cord; his feet are bare and the sand takes them. No marble, no columns, no architecture of any later age.
- Color Language: A narrow, graded palette — cold slate blue in the water and the wet stone, warm ochre where the low sun falls, and the man carried mostly in the ochre and the mid-tones rather than in the blue. ⚠️ **He is not lit apart from the shore** — the same raking light falls on him as on the shingle, and **the shot has no second light for its first person.**
- Texture: Wet shingle with grain and individual stones; coarse wool with visible fibre and a worn seam; skin roughened and marked; the water's surface fine-grained and broken. Film grain present and even.
- Rendering: Photographic — anamorphic optics, a moderate depth of field that holds him and lets the sea go soft behind, subtle oval bokeh, a gentle flare where the light is in frame. **Not a photograph's stillness: a film frame, with a real lens's fall-off at the edges.** No illustration, no CGI look, no cartoon color.
- Visual Density: Low to moderate. He is the single focal point and **nothing in the frame competes with him** — the shore behind him is the shore `s01` already showed, and the sea is a surface, not a subject.
- Time: `日没` — the last of the day, on the island's shore. **The same evening and the same shore as `s01`, `s04`, `s06`, `s08`; the work does not fix a date.**
- Atmosphere: A shore at the hour when the light stops being able to be ignored, and a man who has come out to stand in it.

# 11. MOTION

## Physical Characteristics

- **Weight**: **彼の体は重い。** 足が砂に沈み、**砂のほうが負ける。** ⚠️ **この1本の重さは、歩き方ではなく、足跡に現れる。**

- **Fluidity**: Continuous; no snap, no held cel, no stutter. ⚠️ **この7.58秒に、止まったフレームは一つも無い**——**彼が止まったあとも、波と裾が動いている。**
- **Impact**: **足が砂に当たる。それがこの1本の唯一の衝撃である**——**柔らかい衝撃であり、音は立たない。**
