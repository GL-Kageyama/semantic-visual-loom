# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A bare listening room with no window. Warm-grey walls, no furniture, no props. The room exists so that **nothing competes with the voice** — it has no clock, no writing, and no second figure.

## World Rules

- **Only the voice changes.** The speaker does not leave the spot, does not approach, does not turn.
- **One person.** No listener, no second figure, no reflection, no shadow of anyone else.
- **The line is read once.** It is not repeated, not paraphrased, not continued.
- **No music.** No bed, no chord, no melody, no sting.
- **No writing.** No subtitle, no title, no name, no caption, in any language.
- **No cut.** This ten seconds is one continuous take.
- **It ends on silence.** After the last word the room returns to quiet and the clip holds.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime, held still — one figure against a plain warm-grey wall. **Low visual density**, generous empty space above the shoulders.
- Color Language: the wall's warm grey, the skin's warmth, one cool accent at the collar. **No third hue competes with the face.**
- Texture: clean cel shading at one shadow tone per material, the boundary crisp.
- Rendering: soft volumetric light from frame left, dust suspended and visible against the grey.
- Visual Density: low.
- Time: `時刻を持たない`
- Atmosphere: quiet, level, and composed — **warm without being playful.**

# 3. SUBJECTS

## ミライ

A woman of twenty-seven, standing at the centre of the frame, facing the camera. Her build is straight and settled; her shoulders sit low and still. Her hair is dark, cut to the jaw and tucked behind one ear, with a clean edge. She wears a plain fine-knit top in a soft oatmeal, high at the neck, with a thin dark band at the wrist; no jacket, no pattern, no jewellery except the band.

**Her face is composed, and the expression stays level.** The mouth works for the line and returns to rest; the eyes hold the lens steadily, and a small line at the brow appears once on the second sentence and does not repeat. **She does not smile to soften the sentence, and she does not tilt her head.**

- Behaviour: she speaks as if the room is already quiet and there is no need to fill it; the pace is even and slightly slower than the first candidate.
- Emotional range: calm → a quiet certainty at the end. **No coyness, no wink, no breathy warmth.**

## Voice (for §14 and §18 only — not a visual subject)

**A clear, even voice in the mid-high range, with a firm centre and no chest weight.** Adult and unforced: the pitch sits above a speaking-low and never drops into the chest. **No breathiness, no whisper, no vocal fry, no smile in the low register.** Consonants are clean; the tempo is even, and the ends of sentences are held rather than dropped.

# 7. NARRATIVE

- Beginning: the room is quiet; she is at rest, mouth closed.
- Development: the first sentence, spoken to the lens.
- Turn: a breath, with no words in it.

- End: the mouth closes; the room is quiet again. **Nothing is added after the last word.**

# 9. ACTION

- **Single action: speak once.** Before: mouth closed, at rest. After: mouth closed, at rest, the line completed.
- Speed: even, slightly slower than natural conversational tempo; no acceleration, no rallentando.
- Intensity: moderate — clear, not emphatic.
- **Causes:** the breath at 0–1s causes the first sentence; the breath at 5.6s causes the second. **No other cause enters the frame.**

# 10. CAMERA
