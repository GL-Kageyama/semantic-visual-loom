# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A bare listening room with no window. Warm-grey walls, no furniture, no props. The room exists so that **nothing competes with the voice** — it has no clock, no writing, and no second figure.

## World Rules

- **Only the voice changes.** The speaker does not leave the spot, does not approach, does not turn.
- **One person.** No listener, no second figure, no reflection, no shadow of anyone else.
- **The line is read once.** It is not repeated, not paraphrased, not continued.
- **No music.** No bed, no chord, no melody, no sting.
- **No writing.** No subtitle, no title, no name, no caption, in any language.
- **No cut.** This ten seconds is one continuous take.
- **It ends on silence.** After the last word the room returns to quiet and the clip holds.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Luminous realist anime, held still — one figure against a plain warm-grey wall. **Low visual density**, generous empty space above the shoulders.
- Color Language: the wall's warm grey, the skin's warmth, one cool accent at the collar. **No third hue competes with the face.**
- Texture: clean cel shading at one shadow tone per material, the boundary crisp.
- Rendering: soft volumetric light from frame left, dust suspended and visible against the grey.
- Visual Density: low.
- Time: `時刻を持たない`
- Atmosphere: quiet, level, and warm — **not playful.**

# 3. SUBJECTS

## ミライ

A woman of twenty-two, standing at the centre of the frame, facing the camera. Her build is slight and upright; her shoulders are level and do not square off. Her hair is dark, gathered loosely behind, with a few strands loose at the temple. She wears a plain light shirt in a cool grey-blue, buttoned to the collarbone, no jacket, no jewellery except a small stud at one ear.

**Her face is calm and open, and her expression stays inside a small range.** The mouth is the only part that works: it opens for the line, closes between sentences, and returns to rest. **The eyes hold the lens without widening.** She does not smile at the camera; if a smile arrives, it stays at the corner of the mouth and does not reach the eyes as a performance.

- Behaviour: she speaks as if explaining something to one person in the room, at a natural pace, without pushing.
- Emotional range: calm → a warm sureness at the end of the second sentence. **No coyness, no wink, no head tilt used as punctuation.**

## Voice (for §14 and §18 only — not a visual subject)

**Young adult, light and bright, with open upper harmonics and almost no breath.** It is an adult voice, not a child's: the brightness is in the placement, not in a raised pitch. **No breathiness, no whisper, no vocal fry.** Consonants are clear; the tempo is natural and unhurried.

# 7. NARRATIVE

- **The event:** a woman who was silent speaks one line to camera, and stops.
- Beginning: the room is quiet; she is at rest, mouth closed.
- Development: the first sentence, spoken to the lens.
- Turn: a breath, with no words in it.
- Climax: the second sentence, which names the three places.
- End: the mouth closes; the room is quiet again. **Nothing is added after the last word.**

- Sparse: the first second and the last six tenths.

# 9. ACTION

- **Single action: speak once.** Before: mouth closed, at rest. After: mouth closed, at rest, the line completed.
- Speed: natural speech tempo, no acceleration, no rallentando at the end.
- Intensity: moderate — she is being clear, not emphatic.
- **Causes:** the breath at 0–1s causes the first sentence; the breath at 5.6s causes the second. **No other cause enters the frame.**

# 10. CAMERA

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

## Object Motion

## Environmental Motion

## Physical Characteristics

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The voice comes first.** Intelligibility and timbre outrank beauty of the frame.

3. **Identity holds.** The same face from first frame to last.
4. **The room stays quiet.** No music and no added sound, even if the picture feels bare.
5. **No text.** A burned-in subtitle spoils the sample for its purpose.
