# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A room by the sea, emptied. One window, fogged with salt and condensation.
A cloth crosses it. **Where the cloth goes, light arrives.**

## World Rules

- **The glass is the subject.** What changes is the fog on it.
- **One person only.** Her hand and forearm are the frame; her face is not.
- **The light comes from the window.** No artificial source exists in this room.
- **Nothing is explained.**
- **Nothing supernatural happens.**

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Cinematic still — anamorphic optics, subtle oval bokeh, shallow depth of field, motivated lighting from the window only.
- Color Language: Cold teal interior; the wiped band carries warm amber. **No third family of hue.**
- Texture: Wet cloth on glass, condensation clearing, salt at the frame.
- Rendering: A film frame, not a photograph and not an illustration.
- Visual Density: High at the glass, low everywhere else.
- Time: 夜明け
- Atmosphere: Brightness arriving through an opening the size of a hand.

# 11. MOTION

## Subject Motion

The hand presses and slides. **The pressure is constant**; the pace is not — the first stroke is slow, the second is quicker.

## Object Motion

The cloth gathers and releases at each stroke. **Water thins ahead of it.**

## Environmental Motion

Light floods the band as it opens and widens across the floor. **Fog does not return.**

## Physical Characteristics

- Weight: The cloth has water in it; the pressure is visible.
- Inertia: The push does not correct and does not stop early.
- Acceleration: Slow, then slightly quicker, then still.
- Fluidity: Water on glass; light on a floor.
- Impact: None.

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The band is the subject** — a legible sea is a different shot.
2. **One person.**
3. **The push in is continuous** — no cut, no correction.
4. **The light comes from behind the glass.**
5. Everything else.

## DIRECTION

Play the wipe as an opening, not as cleaning. The cloth crosses a white pane and
leaves a band that holds light; the band widens as the hand works, and the light
falls into the room without ever resolving into a view. Push in slowly and steadily
until the pane fills the frame. Do not explain the room. The band is formal beauty before it is a view: keep the frame still in its choice and the light doing the acting. End on the bright band.
