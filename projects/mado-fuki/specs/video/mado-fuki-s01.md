# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A room by the sea, emptied. One window on the far wall, fogged with salt and
condensation. Outside it is dawn. **The sea is already there and cannot be seen.**

## World Rules

- **The glass is the subject.** The light behind it changes; the view does not.
- **One person only.** Her back and her hands are the frame; her face is not.
- **The light comes from the window.** No artificial source exists in this room.
- **Nothing is explained.** No caption, no sign, no voice names this room.
- **Nothing supernatural happens.**

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Cinematic still — anamorphic optics, subtle oval bokeh, shallow depth of field, motivated lighting from the window only.
- Color Language: Cold teal interior; a warm amber horizon behind the fog. **No third family of hue.**
- Texture: Condensation beads, salt at the frame, bare floorboards.
- Rendering: A film frame, not a photograph and not an illustration.
- Visual Density: Low in the room, high at the glass.
- Time: 夜明け
- Atmosphere: Still, and waiting to be looked through.

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The glass is the subject** — a clear view of the sea is a different shot.
2. **One person.**
3. **The camera does not move.**
4. **The window is matched by the room's perspective** — one-point, converging on the glass.
5. Everything else.

## DIRECTION

Play texture for a held breath. Nothing in the room changes except the light behind
the white glass: it brightens from within and never resolves into a view. Keep the
woman small, seen from behind, cloth in hand, and do not let her lift it. Compose the
room in one-point perspective so that every line in the frame arrives at the window.
Do not explain the room. End on the white glass.
