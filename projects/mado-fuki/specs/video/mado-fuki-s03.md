# 2. WORLD

## World Concept

A room by the sea, emptied. One window. **The sea has been there from the first
frame** — the fog was the only thing between it and the room.

## World Rules

- **The glass is the subject.** What changes is how much of it is still white.
- **One person only.** Her back and her arm are the frame; her face is not.
- **The light comes from the window.**
- **Nothing is explained.**
- **Nothing supernatural happens.** The glass clears because it was wiped.

## Visual Language

- Art Direction: Cinematic still — anamorphic optics, subtle oval bokeh, shallow depth of field, motivated lighting from the window only.
- Color Language: Cold teal interior; the sea is blue-grey with a single amber path on the water. **No third family of hue.**
- Texture: Cleared glass, salt at the frame, floorboards receiving light.
- Rendering: A film frame, not a photograph and not an illustration.
- Visual Density: Low in the room, high at the horizon.
- Time: 夜明け
- Atmosphere: Something that was always there, arriving late.

# 17. GENERATION PRIORITIES

1. **The sea is what arrives** — it is revealed, never entered.
2. **One person.**
3. **The camera moves sideways with her** — never toward the glass.
4. **The horizon stays low and empty.**
5. Everything else.

## DIRECTION

Play the wipe as a reveal that no one announces. The cloth opens the glass in arcs and
colour comes in behind it — first blue, then the amber path on the water — and the sea
resolves while the woman keeps walking along the pane. Travel with her, sideways, at a
steady distance. She stops at the end, her back to us, and the glass hides nothing.
What was hidden becomes seen: discover it, and let no one say so. Do not let her turn. End on the clear glass.
